How to read these

Calculated from the raw ingredients. Where a figure carries a +, anything added to taste is counted as zero, so the real number is a little higher. Each card shows the serving weight. Worth a glance: a 60 g sweet and a 400 g biryani are not the same kind of light.

What “Healthier” means

Lighter than the rest of these recipes: not fried, no refined flour, little added fat or sugar, a sensible meat portion, and built on vegetables, pulses or protein.
