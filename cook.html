Calories

How to read these

Estimates, calculated from the raw ingredients. Anything added to taste is not counted, so the real number is usually a little higher. Check the serving weight too: a 60 g sweet and a 400 g biryani are not light in the same way.

Protein

How these are set

High is at least 20 g a serving and at least 12% of the calories. Medium or better takes anything from 10 g up. Both tests matter: a glass of buttermilk is 23% protein by calories and only 2 g, and a very rich dish can hit 30 g while protein is a tenth of what you are eating.

Most vegetarian dishes land in medium. Dal and rice together make a complete protein, but nothing here can see a plate, only a recipe.

Dietary

What “Healthier” means

Lighter than the rest of these recipes: not fried, no refined flour, a sensible meat portion, built on vegetables, pulses or protein, and at most 20 g fat, 7 g saturated fat and 12 g sugar in a serving.

What “Vegetarian” means

No meat, fish, shellfish or egg, which is what the word usually means in an Indian kitchen. Tick Egg-free on its own if you eat egg.
