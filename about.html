About Khaana

A guide to India's regional kitchens: 536 recipes across 21 cuisines, with the history behind them.

The recipes are not kitchen-tested

Khaana writes them — they are not copied from other recipe sites — and they are checked by machine for internal consistency. But none has been cooked and tasted. Treat them as a researched starting point, not a tested formula, and tell us if one does not work.

Nutrition is estimated

Calculated from the raw ingredients using USDA FoodData Central, not measured in a lab. A + means anything added to taste is counted as zero, so the real figure is a little higher. Sodium is left out, because salting to taste makes it unknowable. If you count for a medical reason, weigh your own.
