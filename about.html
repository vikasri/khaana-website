About

About Khaana

A guide to India's regional kitchens: Recipes across 21 cuisines, with the history behind them.

The recipes

They are written and checked against sources, not cooked in a test kitchen. Times, yields and keeping times are guides rather than tested figures, so trust what is in front of you over what is on the page. Tell us if one does not work.

Allergens

Every recipe is checked against its ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Nothing else is checked, and brands and cross-contamination are beyond what we can see. If you are cooking for someone with an allergy, read the labels yourself.

Food safety

Storage notes follow ordinary guidance. Where a dish is traditionally kept longer than that, the page says so and gives the shorter figure to rely on. None of the keeping times are laboratory tested. Be strict with cooked rice, egg and seafood.

Nutrition is estimated

Calculated from the raw ingredients using USDA FoodData Central. Anything added to taste is not counted, so the real figure is usually a little higher. Sodium is left out, because salting to taste makes it unknowable. If you count for a medical reason, we suggest that you weigh and reestimate your own.
