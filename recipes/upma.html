Tamil Nadu

Upma

soft savoury semolina with crackling dal, ginger and curry leaves

dairy freeegg freehealthierveganvegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook20 min
Total30 min
Serves3
Difficultyeasy

Contains: gluten, tree nuts, mustard

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients

Quantities for 3.

  • 1 cup Ravacoarse bombay rava, not the fine idli variety
  • 2.5 cups, boiling hot Water
  • 1 medium, finely chopped Onion
  • 2, slit lengthwise Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp, finely minced Ginger
  • 1 sprig Curry Leaves
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Urad Dal
  • 1 tsp Chana Dal
  • 10, halved Cashewoptional
  • 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 1/2, juiced Lemonbrightens the whole panoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Dry-roast the rava on low heat for 4 minutes until it smells toasty and moves loosely in the pan, then tip it out.
  • Boil the water and keep it on a low flame. It must go in hot or the upma turns lumpy.
  • Have everything chopped before you start; the tempering to the finish takes six minutes.

Method

  1. Heat the oil and pop the mustard seeds until they jump and quieten.
  2. Add the urad dal and chana dal and fry until both turn a light red-gold, about 90 seconds.The dals are what give upma its crunch; pale dal means no texture.
  3. Add the cashews, green chilli, ginger and curry leaves and fry 30 seconds until the leaves crisp.
  4. Add the onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 4 minutes, no browning.
  5. Pour in the boiling water with the salt and let it come back to a rolling boil.
  6. Lower the heat and add the roasted rava in a thin steady stream with one hand while stirring hard with the other.Dumping it in all at once is exactly how you get lumps.
  7. Keep stirring for 2 minutes until the mixture pulls away from the sides of the pan.
  8. Cover and steam on the lowest heat for 3 minutes, then turn off and rest 2 minutes.
  9. Fluff with a fork, squeeze over the lemon and scatter the coriander.

Storage

Best within a few hours. Refrigerate up to 2 days and revive by steaming with 2 tbsp water sprinkled over; microwaving dries it out.
