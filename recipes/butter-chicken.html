Punjabi

Butter Chicken

Murgh makhani: charred chicken in a tomato and cream gravy, mild and fenugreek-scented

gluten freenut free
Prep25 min
Cook35 min
Total60 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: dairy

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 800g thigh, boneless, in large pieces Chicken
  • 1/2 cup, thick Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp, grated Ginger
  • 8 cloves, crushed Garlic
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri Chillifor colour without heat
  • 1.5 tsp Garam Masala
  • 800g, roughly chopped Tomato
  • 80g Butter
  • 1/2 cup Cream
  • 1 tsp, crushed Kasuri Methidried fenugreek leaves — not the seeds; this is the flavour most people cannot placeoptional
  • 4 pods Cardamomoptional
  • 10, soaked Cashewomit for a nut-free version; the gravy is thinneroptional
  • 1 tsp Sugaroptional
  • 1/2 Lemon
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop, blender, oven

Before you start

  • The dish depends on two things: char on the chicken, and a gravy cooked long enough to lose its raw tomato edge. Neither is optional.
  • Thigh stays juicy through the double cooking; breast will dry out.
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) crushed in at the end is the flavour most people cannot place. If you have it, use it.

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with the yogurt, half the ginger and garlic, the Kashmiri chilli, 1 tsp garam masala, lemon juice and salt. Leave at least 30 minutes, ideally 4 hours refrigerated.
  2. Grill, broil or pan-sear the chicken in a very hot pan until the edges char in places and it is just cooked. Do not cook it through to dryness; it finishes in the sauce. Set aside with any resting juices.
  3. Simmer the tomatoes with the remaining ginger and garlic, the cardamom and a splash of water, covered, for 20 minutes until completely collapsed.
  4. Blend the tomato mixture smooth with the soaked cashews if using, then pass it through a sieve. The sieve is what separates a silky makhani from a rustic curry.Discard the skins and seeds left in the sieve.
  5. Return the puree to the pan with the butter and cook 10 minutes over medium-low heat, stirring, until it darkens and thickens and the butter shows at the edges.
  6. Add the chicken with its juices, the cream, the sugar and the remaining garam masala. Simmer 5 minutes. Crush the kasuri methi between your palms and stir it through. Taste for salt and lemon.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated. Freeze the gravy without the cream for up to 2 months and add cream on reheating; frozen cream sauces can split.
