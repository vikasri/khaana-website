Awadhi/Lucknowi

Boti Kebab

yogurt-marinated mutton cubes, charred outside and juicy through

egg freegluten freehealthiernut free
Prep25 min
Cook20 min
Active45 min
Plus8 hr marinating
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Start the day before. The marinating alone is 8 hr, against 45 min of actual work.

Contains: milk, mustard

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 600g boneless leg, in 3cm cubes Mutton
  • 150g, hung 1 hour in muslin Yogurtthin yogurt slides off the meat
  • 1 tbsp paste Raw Papayatenderiser; skip only if using very young goat
  • 1 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 1.5 tsp Kashmiri Chillicolour without much heat
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 3/4 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1/2 tsp, coarsely crushed Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp, heated to smoking and cooled Mustard Oil
  • 1 tbsp, dry roasted Besanstops the yogurt from weeping in the heat
  • 2 tbsp, for basting Ghee
  • 1, in wedges Lemonoptional
  • 1/2 tsp, to finish Chaat Masalaoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, tawa, oven

Before you start

  • Marinate at least 4 hours, ideally overnight, refrigerated. Under 2 hours and the mutton stays tough.
  • Take the meat out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking so it grills evenly.
  • Roast the besan in a dry pan until it smells nutty and cool it before it goes near the yogurt.

Method

  1. Pat the mutton cubes completely dry and prick each one two or three times with a fork.
  2. Rub in the papaya paste, ginger, garlic and salt and leave 20 minutes.
  3. Whisk the hung yogurt with the roasted besan, kashmiri chilli, coriander powder, garam masala, black pepper and the cooled mustard oil until smooth.Smoked-then-cooled mustard oil loses its raw bite but keeps the pungency.
  4. Fold the meat through the marinade, cover and refrigerate overnight.
  5. Thread onto skewers with a finger gap between cubes so the heat gets all the way round.Packed tight, they steam instead of charring.
  6. Grill over medium-hot charcoal or under an oven grill at 240C for 12–15 minutes, turning every 3 minutes.
  7. Baste with ghee in the last 4 minutes and let the edges blacken in patches.
  8. Rest 3 minutes off the heat, then dust with chaat masala and squeeze over lemon.Resting lets the juices settle; cut one straight away and it bleeds out.

Safe temperatures: Whole cuts of mutton, beef and pork are done at 63°C / 145°F, rested 3 minutes. A long braise passes that well before the meat is tender. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Eat within the hour. Marinated raw meat keeps 2 days refrigerated. Leftover cooked boti is best chopped into a paratha roll rather than reheated whole.
