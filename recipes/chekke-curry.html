Karnataka

Chekke Curry

raw jackfruit simmered soft in a mild ground coconut masala

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep30 min
Cook35 min
Active65 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: mustard

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g, skinned and cut into 3 cm pieces Raw Jackfruitthe pieces should still be firm and white inside
  • 1 cup fresh grated Coconut
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 3 Green Chilli
  • 6 cloves Garlic
  • 3/4 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp, crushed Black Pepper
  • 3 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 2, broken Dried Red Chilli
  • 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 1 medium, finely chopped Onion
  • 2 tsp Vinegarstands in for kachampuli, the dark Coorg sour vinegar; used sparingly and stirred in off the heatoptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 400 ml Water

Cookware

stovetop, pressure cooker, blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Oil your hands and the knife before cutting the jackfruit. The sap is sticky and does not wash off easily.
  • Drop the cut pieces into salted water as you go so they do not brown.
  • Grate the coconut and grind the masala while the jackfruit cooks; it takes about the same time.

Method

  1. Pressure-cook the jackfruit with 250 ml water, the turmeric and 1 tsp salt for 3 whistles, then let the pressure drop by itself.Jackfruit should be soft enough to crush against the pot but still hold its shape. Overcooked, it goes to mush in the gravy.
  2. Grind the coconut, cumin, green chilli and garlic with 150 ml water to a smooth paste.
  3. Heat the coconut oil in a pan, add the mustard seeds and wait for them to pop, about 30 seconds.
  4. Add the dried red chilli and curry leaves and let them crackle for 10 seconds.
  5. Add the onion and fry until soft and pale gold, about 7 minutes.
  6. Stir in the coriander powder and crushed black pepper and fry 1 minute.
  7. Tip in the cooked jackfruit along with its liquid and simmer 5 minutes.
  8. Add the ground coconut paste, bring to a bare simmer and cook 8 minutes, stirring often.Do not let it boil hard once the coconut is in. It will curdle and go grainy.
  9. Check the salt, take the pan off the heat and stir in the vinegar if using. Rest 5 minutes before serving with rice.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat gently with a splash of water. It does not freeze well. The jackfruit turns stringy.
