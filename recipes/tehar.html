Kashmiri

Tehar

turmeric-yellow rice scented with hing and whole spices, the pandit festival plate

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Active35 min
Plus20 min soaking
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 300g, rinsed until the water runs clear Basmati Rice
  • 4 tbsp Gheegenerous ghee is the point of tehar
  • 1.5 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 4 Cloves
  • 1, bruised Black Cardamom
  • 1 stick, 5cm Cinnamonoptional
  • 2 Bay Leafoptional
  • 2, broken Dried Red Chillioptional
  • 1.5 tsp Salt
  • 600ml Water

Cookware

stovetop

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Rinse the rice in four changes of water and soak it 20 minutes, then drain in a sieve for 10 minutes so the grains are damp but not wet.
  • Measure the water exactly. Tehar should be dry and separate, not sticky, and there is no rescuing it once it is wet.
  • Have the turmeric and hing measured into a small bowl together; the tempering takes under a minute.

Method

  1. Heat the ghee in a heavy pan over medium heat and add the cumin seeds, cloves, black cardamom, cinnamon and bay leaf.The cumin should sizzle and darken by a shade within 15 seconds, not sit silently.
  2. Add the broken red chillies and then the asafoetida; it will foam and smell savoury almost at once.Count three seconds and move on. Burnt hing tastes of nothing but bitterness.
  3. Stir in the turmeric off the heat so it blooms in the ghee without scorching, about 10 seconds.
  4. Add the drained rice and turn it gently in the yellow ghee for 2 minutes until every grain is coated and glossy.Use a flat spatula and fold rather than stir; soaked grains snap easily.
  5. Pour in the water, add the salt and bring to a hard boil.
  6. As soon as the surface water has been absorbed and craters appear, about 6 minutes, turn the heat to its lowest setting.
  7. Cover tightly and cook undisturbed for 12 minutes.No lifting the lid. If your lid is loose, weight it with a plate.
  8. Take off the heat and stand covered for a further 10 minutes.
  9. Fluff with a fork, lifting from the base, and serve hot with yogurt or a dry mutton dish.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat covered with 2 tbsp water sprinkled over, on low heat for 6 minutes or in a steamer basket.
