Hyderabadi

Chicken Biryani

marinated chicken layered under part-cooked rice and sealed for the dum

egg freegluten freehealthiernut free
Prep30 min
Cook50 min
Active80 min
Plus4 hr marinating
Serves6
Difficultymoderate

Start the day before. The marinating alone is 4 hr, against 80 min of actual work.

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 1kg bone-in pieces Chickenon the bone: it holds together and gives the rice flavour
  • 500g, soaked 30 minutes Basmati Rice
  • 250g, whisked Yogurt
  • 4, finely sliced and fried brown Onion
  • 2 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 2 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 4, slit Green Chilli
  • a large handful Mint
  • a large handful Coriander Leaves
  • 2 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 1.5 tsp Garam Masala
  • a pinch in 4 tbsp warm milk Saffron
  • 6 Cardamom
  • 2 pieces Cinnamon
  • 5 Cloves
  • 2 Bay Leafoptional
  • 5 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 Lemon
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Marinate for at least 4 hours. The yogurt needs time to work into bone-in chicken, and this is the difference between a biryani and rice with chicken in it.
  • Weigh the rice and the water. Biryani is unforgiving of guesswork.

Method

  1. Fry the onions slowly in ghee until deep brown and crisp. Drain and reserve.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger, garlic, chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, half the birista, half the herbs, lemon juice and salt. Refrigerate 4 hours.
  3. Bring the chicken to room temperature and spread it in a heavy pot with 3 tbsp ghee and the slit chillies.
  4. Boil the rice in well-salted water with the whole spices until 70 per cent done, then drain immediately.Drain the second it is ready. Thirty seconds too long and the finished biryani is mush.
  5. Layer the rice over the raw chicken and top with the remaining birista, herbs, saffron milk and ghee.
  6. Seal the pot with a tight lid or foil. Cook 6 minutes on high, then 35 minutes on the lowest heat.
  7. Rest 15 minutes before opening, then lift from the bottom so each plate gets rice and chicken.The rest lets the steam settle. Opening early releases it and the top layer dries.

Safe temperatures: Chicken and duck are done at 74°C / 165°F, taken at the thickest part and clear of the bone. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat covered with a splash of water. Biryani freezes poorly: the rice turns to paste.
