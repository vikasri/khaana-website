Rajasthani

Pyaaz Kachori

flaky fried pastry packed with a dark, heavily spiced onion and besan filling

dairy freeegg freenut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep35 min
Cook30 min
Active65 min
Plus30 min resting
Serves4
Difficultyadvanced

Contains: gluten

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 250g Maida
  • 60ml for the dough, plus 700ml for frying Neutral Oilthe dough fat is what makes the crust flake
  • 400g (about 4 medium), finely chopped Onion
  • 3 tbsp Besanbinds the filling and stops it going watery
  • 2 tsp, crushed Fennel Seeds
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1.5 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Amchur
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetida
  • 1 tbsp, grated Gingeroptional
  • 2, finely chopped Green Chillioptional
  • 1/2 tsp Nigellaoptional
  • about 110ml Water
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

kadhai, stovetop

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Rub the oil into the flour until a fistful holds its shape when squeezed. If it crumbles apart, add more oil. This test decides whether the kachori flakes or goes hard.
  • Make the filling first and cool it completely. Warm filling steams inside the pastry and bursts it in the oil.
  • Rest the dough 30 minutes under a damp cloth before rolling.

Method

  1. Rub the 60ml oil into the maida with 1/2 tsp salt for 3 minutes, then add water a little at a time to make a soft, smooth dough. Cover and rest 30 minutes.Softer than roti dough. A tight dough gives a hard, blistered kachori.
  2. Heat 3 tbsp oil in a kadhai and crackle the cumin, fennel and nigella, then add the hing.
  3. Add the chopped onion and cook 12-15 minutes over medium heat until it loses all its water and turns light brown.Keep going until the pan is dry. Wet filling is the single most common reason kachoris split.
  4. Stir in the ginger, green chilli and besan and roast 4 minutes until the besan smells nutty.
  5. Add the ground spices, amchur and salt and bhuno 3 more minutes, then spread the filling on a plate to cool.
  6. Divide the dough into 8, roll each into a ball, and flatten into a 8cm disc thicker at the centre than the edge.
  7. Place a heaped tablespoon of filling in the middle, gather the edges over it, pinch shut and pull off the excess knot of dough.
  8. Flatten each stuffed ball gently with your palm into a 7cm disc. Rest them 10 minutes, seam down.Press with the palm, not a rolling pin. A pin tears the pastry and the filling leaks.
  9. Heat the frying oil to just 140C, low enough that a kachori dropped in takes 8-10 seconds to float. Slide in 3 or 4.
  10. Fry very slowly, 12-14 minutes, spooning oil over the tops, until they are covered in blisters and pale gold. Raise the heat for the last 2 minutes to crisp them.Low and slow is what makes the layers. Hot oil browns the outside and leaves raw dough inside.
  11. Drain and serve warm, cracked open with tamarind chutney poured in.

Storage

Keeps 2 days in an airtight tin at room temperature. Re-crisp in a 180C oven for 6-8 minutes. The filling alone freezes for a month.
