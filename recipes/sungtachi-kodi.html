Sungtachi Kodi

everyday prawn and drumstick curry in a thin red coconut masala

dairy freeegg freegluten freehealthiernut freepescatarian
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Active45 min
Plus15 min resting
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: crustacean shellfish

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 400g medium, shelled and deveined Prawns
  • 2, in 6cm lengths Drumstickadds the sweetness that makes this curry
  • 1.5 cups grated fresh Coconut
  • 8 dried, stalks removed Kashmiri Chillifor colour more than heat
  • 1 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 3/4 tsp Turmeric
  • 5 cloves Garlic
  • 1 medium, finely chopped Onion
  • 4 petals Kokumor 1 tbsp tamarind pulp
  • 2, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 2 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, blender

Before you start

  • Toss the prawns with 1/4 tsp turmeric and a pinch of salt and leave 15 minutes, then rinse and drain well.
  • Rinse the kokum petals and soak them in 3 tbsp warm water while you grind the masala.
  • Peel the drumsticks with a knife, leaving the tender inner rib. The tough outer skin never softens.

Method

  1. Grind the coconut, Kashmiri chillies, coriander powder, cumin, turmeric and garlic with 250ml warm water to a completely smooth paste.Blend for a full four minutes. A gritty masala is the commonest fault in a home coconut curry.
  2. Boil the drumstick pieces in 250ml salted water for 8 minutes, until a fingernail marks the flesh easily. Keep the water.
  3. Heat the coconut oil in a wide pan and soften the onion 5 minutes until translucent.
  4. Pour in the ground masala along with the drumstick and its cooking water, add the green chillies and salt, and bring to a simmer.
  5. Simmer 8 minutes, stirring now and then, until the raw smell of the coconut has gone and the curry looks glossy rather than chalky.Do not let it reach a rolling boil once the coconut is in, it will split.
  6. Add the kokum with its soaking water and simmer 2 minutes.
  7. Slide in the prawns and cook 3-4 minutes only, until they curl and turn opaque.Prawns go from done to rubbery in about a minute. Pull the pan as soon as they curve into a C.
  8. Take off the heat, check the salt and sourness, and rest 5 minutes before serving with rice.

Safe temperatures: Prawns, crab and squid are done when the flesh is opaque and pearly right through. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated, though the prawns firm up. Reheat over low heat just to steaming. Do not freeze.
