Pithla

loose gram flour curry slackened with sour buttermilk, garlicky and made in ten minutes

egg freegluten freevegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Active25 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk, peanuts, mustard

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 75g Besan
  • 400ml, sour Buttermilkthe sourness is the point; leave fresh buttermilk out overnight
  • 8 cloves, crushed Garlic
  • 4, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 small, finely chopped Onionoptional
  • 3 tbsp Groundnut Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 10 Curry Leaves
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-freeoptional
  • 150ml Water
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Whisk the besan into the cold buttermilk before anything goes on the heat. Lumps formed in a hot pan never come out.
  • Sour buttermilk gives pithla its character. Leave fresh buttermilk on the counter overnight, or stir a squeeze of lemon into it.
  • Have the bhakri dough ready if you are eating it the proper way; pithla waits for nobody.

Method

  1. Whisk the besan into the cold buttermilk with the turmeric and salt until completely smooth, then whisk in the water.Push the slurry through a sieve if you see any lumps. Thirty seconds now saves the dish.
  2. Heat the oil, pop the mustard seeds, then add the cumin, curry leaves and hing.
  3. Add the garlic and green chilli and fry 1 minute until the garlic smells sweet but has not coloured.
  4. Add the onion, if using, and cook 4 minutes until soft.
  5. Pour in the besan slurry in a slow stream, stirring continuously.
  6. Bring to a gentle boil and simmer 8 to 10 minutes, stirring often, until it thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon and the raw besan smell is gone.It thickens a lot as it cools, so pull it off looser than you want to serve it.
  7. Check the salt, fold through the coriander and pour into bowls.
  8. Serve immediately with hot jowar bhakri, raw onion and a green chilli.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated but sets firm. Reheat over low heat with 3 to 4 tbsp water, whisking, until it loosens back to a pouring consistency.
