Lemon Rice

yellow rice cut with lemon, peanuts and split dals for crunch

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freehealthier
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Total25 min
Serves3
Difficultyeasy

Contains: nuts

Ingredients

Quantities for 3.

  • 1 cup raw, cooked and spread out to cool Ricegrains must be separate and cold
  • 2, juiced, about 4 tbsp Lemon
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 3 tbsp Groundnut Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tbsp Chana Dal
  • 1 tsp Urad Dal
  • 3 tbsp, raw Peanut
  • 2, broken Dried Red Chilli
  • 2, slit Green Chilli
  • 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 1 tsp, minced Gingeroptional
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Cook the rice with 1.75 cups water so it stays firm, then spread it on a wide plate to cool completely, 20 minutes.
  • Juice the lemons and keep the juice off the heat until the very end.
  • Rub a teaspoon of oil through the cooled rice with your fingers to loosen any clumps.

Method

  1. Heat the oil and pop the mustard seeds.
  2. Add the peanuts and fry 2 minutes until they darken a shade and smell roasted.
  3. Add the chana dal and urad dal and fry until golden, about 1 minute.Chana dal takes longer than urad, so it goes in first.
  4. Add the red chilli, green chilli, curry leaves and hing and let the leaves crackle for 15 seconds.
  5. Stir in the turmeric and take the pan off the heat immediately.Turmeric burns bitter in about ten seconds on a hot pan.
  6. Add the lemon juice and salt to the warm tempering and swirl to combine.
  7. Tip in the cooled rice and fold gently with a flat spatula until every grain is yellow.Fold, do not stir. Stirring mashes the grains into paste.
  8. Rest 10 minutes off the heat for the flavour to soak in, then scatter the coriander and serve.

Storage

Keeps a day at room temperature and 2 days refrigerated. Serve at room temperature; reheating dulls the lemon.
