Parsi

Bhida Par Eeda

okra fried dry with onion and eggs cracked over the top

vegetariangluten freedairy freenut free
Prep15 min
Cook25 min
Total40 min
Serves3
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 3.

  • 400g, wiped dry and cut into 2cm rounds Okra
  • 4 large Eggs
  • 2 medium, finely sliced Onion
  • 2, finely chopped Green Chilli
  • 4 cloves, finely chopped Garlic
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 3/4 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1/2, juiced Lemonoptional
  • a handful, chopped Coriander Leaves
  • 4 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 1/2 tsp, coarsely ground Black Pepper
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Wash the okra a good hour ahead and dry every pod with a cloth. Any water left on them turns the dish slimy.
  • Use a knife and board that are completely dry for the same reason.
  • Bring the eggs to room temperature.

Method

  1. Heat 3 tbsp of the oil in a wide kadhai and fry the okra over high heat, undisturbed for the first 3 minutes so it sears rather than steams.Do not stir constantly and do not cover the pan. Both make it slimy.
  2. Keep frying 8-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until the pieces are browned at the edges and no longer sticky. Lift them out onto a plate.
  3. Add the last tbsp of oil and fry the onion until golden, 8 minutes.
  4. Add the garlic and green chilli and fry 1 minute.
  5. Stir in turmeric, chilli powder, cumin and coriander powder for 30 seconds.
  6. Return the okra with salt, toss 2 minutes to coat, then spread it into an even layer.
  7. Make four hollows and crack in the eggs. Season with salt and black pepper.
  8. Cover and cook on low 7-9 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks still run.If your lid does not fit well, add a tablespoon of water at the pan edge to raise steam.
  9. Squeeze over the lemon, scatter the coriander leaves and serve hot with rotli.

Storage

Eat the same day. The okra will not stay crisp; leftovers keep 1 day refrigerated and are best reheated uncovered in a hot pan.
