Neem Tulsi Shot

the bitterest of the lot, taken in spring

dairy freeegg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarianwellness shot
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook0 min
Active10 min
Serves1
Difficultyeasy

Allergens in Neem Tulsi Shot: none of the ten listed below.

Ingredients

Quantities for 1.

  • 15 tender leaves Neemyoung leaves only; old ones are unbearable
  • 15 leaves Tulsithe tulsi is there to make the neem possible
  • 2 tsp Honey
  • ½, juiced Lemon
  • a pinch Black Pepperoptional

Cookware

blender

Ingredient names in English →

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Method

  1. Wash the neem and tulsi leaves well.
  2. Blend with 100ml water until the leaves break down entirely.
  3. Strain twice through muslin. Any leaf fragment left in will make it worse.
  4. Stir in the honey, lemon and pepper. Drink in one go; it does not improve with sipping.

Storage

Same day. Neem juice darkens and turns harsher within hours.
