Basanti Pulao

sweet saffron-yellow ghee rice studded with cashews and raisins

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicvegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Active45 min
Plus30 min resting
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, tree nuts

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 300g Basmati Ricegobindobhog is traditional; basmati works if you shorten the cooking by 3 minutes
  • 4 tbsp Ghee
  • 3/4 tsp Turmericthis, not the saffron, is what makes it yellow
  • a large pinch, soaked in 2 tbsp warm water Saffronoptional
  • 15, halved Cashew
  • 2 tbsp Raisins
  • 2 Bay Leaf
  • 5, bruised Cardamom
  • 3cm stick Cinnamon
  • 4 Cloves
  • 1 tsp, grated Gingeroptional
  • 2 tbsp Sugar
  • 1.5 tsp Salt
  • 500ml, hot Water

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Wash the rice gently in three changes of water until it runs clear, then drain and spread it on a cloth for 30 minutes until the grains are dry to the touch.
  • Toss the dried rice with 2 tbsp of the ghee, the turmeric and 1 tsp salt and leave it 30 minutes. This coats each grain and keeps them separate.
  • Have the water hot on the side. Adding cold water to hot rice makes it break.

Method

  1. Heat the remaining 2 tbsp ghee in a heavy pan and fry the cashews until golden, about 90 seconds. Lift out.Cashews go from gold to burnt in about ten seconds. Watch them.
  2. Fry the raisins in the same ghee for 20 seconds until they swell, then lift them out too.
  3. Add the bay leaves, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves and let them puff for 20 seconds.
  4. Add the grated ginger and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Tip in the marinated rice and fry very gently for 3 minutes, folding rather than stirring.Turmeric-coated rice grains are fragile. A flat spoon and slow folds keep them whole.
  6. Pour in 500ml hot water with the sugar, the saffron and its soaking water, and the rest of the salt.
  7. Bring to a boil, then cover tightly and cook on the lowest possible heat for 12 minutes.Do not lift the lid. Every peek lets out the steam that is cooking the top layer.
  8. Turn off the heat and leave it covered and undisturbed for 10 minutes.
  9. Uncover, fork through the fried cashews and raisins, and serve with kosha mangsho or a dry vegetable.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat covered with 2 tbsp water in a pan over low heat, or steam it. The microwave dries the grains out.
