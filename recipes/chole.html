Punjabi

Chole

Punjabi chickpea curry, dark and tangy with pomegranate and dried mango

vegetarianvegangluten freenut freedairy freehealthier
Prep15 min
Cook40 min
Total55 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1.5 cups dried, soaked overnight Chickpeasor 3 tins, drained
  • 2 large, finely chopped Onion
  • 3, pureed Tomato
  • 2 tbsp, grated Ginger
  • 6 cloves, crushed Garlic
  • 2, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 Bay Leafoptional
  • 1 Black Cardamomthe smoky note that makes chole taste like choleoptional
  • 1 Lemon
  • a handful Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop, pressure cooker

Before you start

  • The dark colour of restaurant chole comes from cooking the chickpeas with a black tea bag or a piece of dried amla. Neither is required, but if you want the colour, add a tea bag to the cooking water and remove it before draining.
  • If using tinned chickpeas, simmer them in the finished gravy for 15 minutes so they absorb it; they will otherwise taste separate from the sauce.

Method

  1. Pressure cook the soaked chickpeas with salt and the black cardamom in fresh water for 5-6 whistles, until a chickpea crushes easily between finger and thumb. Reserve the cooking liquid.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pan. Add the cumin seeds and bay leaf, and when the cumin sizzles, add the onion. Fry patiently over medium heat until genuinely brown, 10-12 minutes; this is most of the flavour.
  3. Add the ginger and garlic and cook for a minute until the raw smell goes, then add the tomato puree, turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder.
  4. Cook the masala down until the oil separates and pools at the edges of the pan, 8-10 minutes. Do not rush this.
  5. Add the drained chickpeas with a cup of the reserved cooking liquid. Simmer uncovered 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas against the side of the pan to thicken the gravy.
  6. Stir in the garam masala, a good squeeze of lemon and the coriander leaves. Rest 10 minutes off the heat before serving; chole improves noticeably in that time.

Storage

Better on the second day. Keeps 4 days refrigerated, freezes 2 months. Reheat gently with a splash of water.
