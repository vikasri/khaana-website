Bihari

Dahi Chura Gud

beaten rice softened in cold yogurt with jaggery, the Makar Sankranti breakfast

egg freegluten freenut freevegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook0 min
Active10 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 200g, thick variety Pohathin poha dissolves; you need the thick flakes
  • 600g, thick and well chilled Yogurtsweet-set dahi, not sour
  • 150g, in small pieces or grated Jaggery
  • 100 ml, cold Milkloosens it if your yogurt is very thickoptional
  • 2 tbsp Sugaronly if the jaggery is mildoptional

Cookware

Before you start

  • Rinse the poha in a colander under cold running water for 15 seconds, then leave it to drain for 10 minutes. That soak is all the softening it gets.
  • Chill the yogurt properly; this dish is eaten cold and lukewarm dahi makes it heavy.
  • Break the jaggery into small shards rather than one lump so every mouthful gets some.

Method

  1. Rinse the thick poha in a colander under cold water and shake it dry.Fifteen seconds under the tap, no longer. Poha left sitting in water turns to paste.
  2. Let it drain and swell for 10 minutes; the flakes should be soft enough to crush between two fingers but still separate.
  3. Whisk the chilled yogurt smooth in a large bowl with a pinch of salt.
  4. Loosen it with the cold milk if it is stiff. You want the consistency of thin cream.
  5. Fold the drained poha into the yogurt and leave it 5 minutes to absorb.Fold, do not beat. The flakes should stay recognisable.
  6. Taste and stir in the sugar only if the mixture is flat.
  7. Serve into bowls with a generous pile of jaggery shards on the side of each portion, to be broken off and eaten with each spoonful.The jaggery goes alongside, not stirred in. The contrast of cold sour dahi and sharp sweet gud is the dish.

Storage

Assemble and eat within an hour; the poha keeps swelling and the yogurt turns sour. Soaked poha can be drained and refrigerated for a few hours ahead, but mix in the yogurt only at the table.
