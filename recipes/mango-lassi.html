Mango Lassi

ripe mango blended with yogurt into a thick, cold drink

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Prep5 min
Cook0 min
Active5 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 2 ripe, flesh only, about 350g Raw Mangoripe and sweet, not the sour pickling kind
  • 300g, cold Yogurt
  • 100ml, cold Milkoptional
  • 2 tbsp, or to taste Sugar
  • 2, seeds ground Cardamomoptional
  • a few strands Saffronoptional
  • a pinch Saltlifts the mangooptional

Cookware

blender

Before you start

  • Chill everything first. Ice thins the drink; cold ingredients keep it thick.
  • Taste the mango before you add sugar. A good ripe mango may need none at all.

Method

  1. Cut the mango flesh away from the stone and chill it, along with the yogurt and milk.
  2. Blend the mango to a smooth puree on its own first.On its own, so any fibres break down before the yogurt is added.
  3. Add the cold yogurt, milk, sugar and the pinch of salt, and blend briefly.Briefly. Over-blending yogurt makes it thin and frothy.
  4. Taste and adjust the sugar.
  5. Pour into chilled glasses and finish with ground cardamom and a few saffron strands.

Storage

Drink within a few hours. It separates in the fridge; whisk to bring it back. Freezes well as kulfi-style lollies.
