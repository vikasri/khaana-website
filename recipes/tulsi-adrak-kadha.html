Tulsi Adrak Kadha

the warm one: holy basil and ginger, simmered rather than blended

dairy freeegg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarianwellness shot
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep5 min
Cook15 min
Active20 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Allergens in Tulsi Adrak Kadha: none of the ten listed below.

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 15 fresh leaves Tulsidried tulsi works at half the quantity
  • 20g fresh, sliced Ginger
  • 4 peppercorns, cracked Black Pepper
  • 1 tsp Honeystirred in off the heat, not into boiling liquidoptional
  • a squeeze Lemonoptional

Cookware

stovetop

Ingredient names in English →

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Method

  1. Bring 300ml water to the boil with the ginger and peppercorns.
  2. Lower the heat and simmer ten minutes, until the water has taken colour and reduced by about a third.
  3. Take off the heat, add the tulsi leaves and cover. Leave five minutes.
  4. Strain into two cups. Stir in the honey and lemon once it has stopped steaming hard.

Storage

Best fresh. It will reheat once without much loss.
