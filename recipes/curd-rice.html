Tamil Nadu

Curd Rice

cooling mashed rice set in yogurt with a mustard and chilli tempering

egg freegluten freehealthierno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Active25 min
Serves3
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk, mustard

Ingredients

Quantities for 3.

  • 3/4 cup raw, cooked very soft Rice
  • 1.5 cups, fresh and thick, whisked smooth Yogurt
  • 1/2 cup, cold Milkkeeps it from souring and loosens the set
  • 2, finely minced Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp, finely minced Ginger
  • 1 sprig Curry Leaves
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Urad Dal
  • 1, broken Dried Red Chilli
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 2 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 3 tbsp, seeds Pomegranateoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Cook the rice with 2.5 cups water (twice the usual) so it collapses easily.
  • Let the rice cool to just warm before the yogurt goes in; hot rice curdles it.
  • Whisk the yogurt until it is completely lump-free.

Method

  1. Mash the warm cooked rice against the side of the pot with the back of a ladle until no whole grains remain.The mash is what makes curd rice creamy rather than a rice salad.
  2. Stir in the milk and salt and let it stand 5 minutes to cool further.
  3. Fold in the whisked yogurt, the minced ginger and green chilli.
  4. Check the consistency. It should be loose and pourable, since it thickens as it sits.Add a splash more milk if it looks like a mound rather than a pool.
  5. Heat the oil in a small pan and pop the mustard seeds.
  6. Add the urad dal, fry to pale gold, then add the red chilli, curry leaves and hing off the heat.
  7. Pour the tempering over the curd rice and fold it through once.
  8. Top with pomegranate seeds and coriander and serve cool, not chilled.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated but turns sour after that. Bring back to room temperature and slacken with 2 tbsp milk before serving; never heat it.
