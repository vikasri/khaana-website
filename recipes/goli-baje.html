Goli Baje

soft, tangy gram flour dumplings from the Mangalore coast

egg freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep15 min
Cook25 min
Active40 min
Plus2 hr fermenting
Serves20
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk, gluten

Ingredients

Quantities for 20.

  • 200g Maida
  • 60g Besan
  • 200g, sour Yogurtsour curd is what gives goli baje its tang and its lift
  • 4, chopped Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp, chopped Ginger
  • 2 sprigs, torn Curry Leaves
  • 3 tbsp, cut into slivers Coconut
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Soda
  • 500ml for deep frying Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Also sold as Mangalore bajji. The batter must be thick enough to hold a rough ball dropped from the fingers.
  • The batter needs a rest of at least 2 hours. That fermentation is the difference between light and leaden.

Method

  1. Whisk the yogurt smooth and mix in both flours, salt and the soda into a thick batter.
  2. Rest, covered, 2 to 3 hours at room temperature until it looks aerated.
  3. Fold in the green chilli, ginger, curry leaves and coconut slivers.
  4. Drop rough balls into medium-hot oil using wet fingers.Medium heat. Too hot and they brown before the middle sets.
  5. Fry 5 minutes, turning, until deep gold all over. Drain and serve hot with coconut chutney.

Storage

Eat within the hour. They deflate and toughen as they cool and do not reheat convincingly.
