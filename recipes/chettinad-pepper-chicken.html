Chettinad Pepper Chicken

semi-dry milagu kozhi varuval, hot with coarsely cracked black pepper

dairy freeegg freegluten freehealthiernut free
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: sesame

Checked against the ingredient list for milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Nothing else is checked, and brands vary, so read the label on anything you have not bought before.

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 700g boneless thigh, cut into 3cm pieces Chicken
  • 2 tbsp, coarsely cracked Black Peppercrack it in a mortar, not a fine grinder
  • 1 tsp Fennel Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 12 small, thinly sliced Shallot
  • 8 cloves, crushed Garlic
  • 1 inch piece, finely chopped Ginger
  • 3 sprigs Curry Leavestwo in the oil, one fried crisp for the top
  • 3, broken Dried Red Chilli
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 3 tbsp Sesame Oilgingelly oil
  • 1/2, juiced Lemonoptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 100ml Water
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesto finishoptional

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Marinate the chicken with the turmeric, 1 tbsp of the cracked pepper and 1 tsp salt for 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
  • Crack the peppercorns coarsely by hand. Fine pepper powder goes acrid in the pan and you lose the pop of whole pieces.
  • This dish moves fast once the pan is hot, so slice the shallots and pick the curry leaves before you start.

Method

  1. Heat the gingelly oil in a kadhai until it shimmers, then add the fennel, cumin and broken red chillies.
  2. Add two sprigs of curry leaves and stand back. They will crackle hard for 10 seconds.
  3. Tip in the shallots and fry 6 minutes over medium-high heat until browned at the edges.Brown, not soft. The sweetness of caramelised shallot is what balances all that pepper.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger and fry 90 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Stir in the coriander powder and cook 30 seconds, just until it darkens.
  6. Add the marinated chicken and fry on high for 5 minutes, turning, until the pieces are opaque and catching colour.
  7. Pour in 100ml water, cover and cook 10 minutes on medium until the chicken is done through.
  8. Uncover, add the remaining 1 tbsp cracked pepper and stir-fry 5-6 minutes on high until the liquid has gone and the masala is a dark clinging coat.Adding half the pepper at the end keeps its heat sharp and aromatic rather than muddy.
  9. Fry the last sprig of curry leaves in the residual oil at the side of the pan for 20 seconds until crisp.
  10. Squeeze over the lemon, scatter the crisp curry leaves and coriander, and serve hot with rice or parotta.

Storage

Best eaten the day it is made. Keeps 2 days refrigerated; revive in a hot dry pan for 3 minutes rather than adding water.
