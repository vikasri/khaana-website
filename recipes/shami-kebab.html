Awadhi/Lucknowi

Shami Kebab

dense little mutton and chana dal patties, smooth inside and crisp at the edge

gluten freenut freehealthier
Prep25 min
Cook50 min
Total75 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: dairy

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g boneless shoulder, cut in 3cm cubes Mutton
  • 100g, rinsed Chana Dalthe binder — nothing else does the same job
  • 1 medium quartered for the boil, 1 small very finely chopped for the mix Onion
  • 8 cloves Garlic
  • 2 inch piece, sliced Ginger
  • 4 whole Dried Red Chilli
  • 1 Black Cardamom
  • 4 green Cardamom
  • 1 inch stick Cinnamon
  • 4 Cloves
  • 1 tsp whole Black Pepper
  • 250ml Water
  • 1, beaten Eggshelps the patties hold when fryingoptional
  • 2, finely chopped Green Chillioptional
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 1 tbsp, chopped Mintoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1, in wedges Lemonto squeeze at the tableoptional
  • 4 tbsp, for shallow frying Gheeneutral oil works but tastes flatter
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop, pressure cooker, blender, tawa

Before you start

  • Soak the chana dal 1 hour in warm water. Undersoaked dal stays gritty and the kebab will crumble.
  • The cooked mince must be bone dry before grinding — any moisture and the patties fall apart in the pan.
  • Chill the shaped kebabs 30 minutes before frying. They firm up and turn far more easily.

Method

  1. Pressure cook the mutton, drained dal, quartered onion, garlic, ginger, whole chillies, black cardamom, green cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, peppercorns, salt and 250ml water for 5 whistles, about 20 minutes.In a pan instead, allow 50 minutes covered on low.
  2. Open the lid and boil hard, stirring, until every trace of liquid has gone and the mix looks dry and sandy, 8–10 minutes.This is the step people skip. Wet mince equals kebabs that disintegrate.
  3. Cool completely, then pick out the cinnamon stick and black cardamom shell and grind the rest to a smooth, tight paste with no added water.
  4. Knead in the finely chopped onion, green chilli, coriander, mint, garam masala and the beaten egg. Taste for salt now.
  5. Divide into 12 and shape into flat discs 5cm across and 1.5cm thick. Refrigerate 30 minutes.
  6. Heat 2 tbsp ghee on a tawa over medium heat and lay in the kebabs without crowding.
  7. Fry 3–4 minutes a side until a deep brown crust forms, adding the remaining ghee as you turn.Turn once only, with a thin spatula. Fiddling breaks them.
  8. Drain on paper, rest 2 minutes and serve hot with lemon wedges and sliced onion.

Storage

Cooked kebabs keep 3 days refrigerated and reheat well on a dry tawa over low heat. The raw ground mix freezes for a month; thaw fully before shaping.
