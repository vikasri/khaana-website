Bengali

Kosha Mangsho

Mutton slow-fried in its own masala until almost dry and very dark

gluten freenut free
Prep20 min
Cook90 min
Total110 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: dairy

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 750g on the bone Mutton
  • 1/2 cup Yogurt
  • 4, sliced Onion
  • 2 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 2 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 2 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 2 Bay Leafoptional
  • 2 pieces Cinnamonoptional
  • 4 Cardamomoptional
  • 1 tsp Sugarfor colour, a Bengali habitoptional
  • 5 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 2, halved and fried Potatooptional but expected in Bengaloptional
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop

Before you start

  • Kosha means to fry slowly and persistently. The dish is defined by that process, not by a sauce — you are looking for near-dry meat coated in dark masala.
  • Marinate the mutton in yogurt and turmeric for at least an hour; overnight is better.

Method

  1. Marinate the mutton with yogurt, turmeric and salt for an hour.
  2. Heat the mustard oil to smoking, cool slightly, then caramelise the sugar until it darkens — this is where the colour comes from.Seconds, not minutes. Burnt sugar is bitter.
  3. Add the whole spices and sliced onion and fry until genuinely brown, 12-15 minutes.
  4. Add ginger, garlic and the powdered spices, then the marinated mutton. Now begin koshano: fry over medium-low heat, stirring often, for 30-40 minutes.Add a splash of hot water whenever it catches, and keep frying. This is the whole recipe.
  5. When the oil separates and the meat is deep brown, add just enough hot water to cover, cover the pan and simmer 30-40 minutes until tender.
  6. Add the fried potatoes for the last 10 minutes, finish with garam masala, and reduce until the gravy clings.

Storage

Better the next day. Keeps 3 days refrigerated, freezes 2 months.
