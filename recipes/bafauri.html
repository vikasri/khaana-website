Bafauri

steamed chana dal dumplings, the light cousin of the fried snack

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut freeveganvegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Active50 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 250g, soaked 4 hours Chana Dal
  • 1, finely chopped Onion
  • 4, chopped Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp, grated Ginger
  • a handful Coriander Leaves
  • 1/2 tsp Ajwain
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • a pinch Asafoetida
  • 1 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

steamer, blender

Before you start

  • Bafauri is Chhattisgarh's before it is anyone else's, and it is made across the Bhojpuri belt and Madhya Pradesh too. Steamed rather than fried is the whole point of it.
  • Steamed, not fried, which makes it the rare Bihari snack you can eat a lot of.
  • Grind the dal coarse. A smooth paste steams into something rubbery.

Method

  1. Drain the dal and grind coarse with almost no water. It should still have grit to it.
  2. Mix in the onion, green chilli, ginger, coriander, ajwain, turmeric, asafoetida, oil and salt.
  3. Shape into balls the size of a lime with wet hands.
  4. Steam 20 minutes until firm and a skewer comes out clean.Steam, do not boil. Boiled bafauri falls apart.
  5. Rest 5 minutes before lifting them off, and serve with a sharp chutney.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Re-steam for 5 minutes to soften rather than microwaving, which toughens them.
