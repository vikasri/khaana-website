Sev Puri

flat crisp puris loaded with potato, chutneys and a mound of fine sev

dairy freeegg freenut freeveganvegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Total35 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: gluten

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 200g Maidafor the flat puris
  • 300g, boiled and diced small Potato
  • 1, finely chopped Onion
  • 100g Besanfor sev
  • 5 tbsp sweet extract Tamarind
  • a large handful Mint
  • a large handful Coriander Leaves
  • 2 Green Chilli
  • 2 tsp Chaat Masala
  • 1 tsp Black Salt
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 Lemon
  • 500ml Neutral Oilfor deep frying
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Sev puri uses the flat papdi, not the hollow shell. Pricking the discs before frying is what keeps them flat.
  • The sev should be fine, not thick. Thick sev turns the dish chewy.

Method

  1. Make a stiff maida dough with 2 tbsp oil and cold water. Rest 20 minutes.
  2. Roll thin, cut 5cm discs, prick all over, and fry at 160C until hard and barely coloured.
  3. Blend mint, coriander, chilli and lemon into a thick green chutney.
  4. Fry fine sev from a stiff besan batter, or use bought.
  5. Lay the puris out, top each with potato and onion.
  6. Spoon on tamarind chutney and green chutney, dust with chaat masala, black salt and chilli powder.
  7. Bury the lot in sev and serve at once.Sev last, and generously. It is the name of the dish.

Storage

Puris and sev keep two weeks airtight. Assembled, they last about a minute.
