Anglo-Indian

Coconut Toffee

coconut and milk boiled to a fudge, cut in diamonds at Christmas

egg freegluten freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook35 min
Active45 min
Serves20
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk, tree nuts

Ingredients

Quantities for 20.

  • 300g, freshly grated Coconut
  • 400g Sugar
  • 200ml Milk
  • 40g Butter
  • 6 pods, seeds ground Cardamom
  • 50g, chopped Cashewoptional

Cookware

stovetop

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • This is the Anglo-Indian Christmas sweet, made alongside kul kuls and rose cookies and given away in tins.
  • It is a sugar-stage recipe. Stop at the wrong moment and you get either syrup or a brick.

Method

  1. Boil the sugar and milk together until the sugar dissolves completely.
  2. Add the coconut and cook on medium, stirring constantly, for 20 minutes.Constantly. The bottom catches the moment you look away.
  3. Add the butter and cardamom and keep going until the mixture leaves the sides of the pan in one mass and a drop set in cold water holds its shape.
  4. Pour into a greased tray, scatter the cashew and press down flat.
  5. Mark into diamonds while still warm and cut through when cold.Mark warm, cut cold. Cutting it hot smears it; cutting it fully cold shatters it.

Storage

Keeps 2 weeks in an airtight tin at room temperature. It dries out slowly rather than spoiling, so eat it while it still yields.
