Punjabi

Matar Paneer

peas and paneer in a straightforward onion-tomato gravy, the weeknight standard

vegetariangluten freenut freehealthier
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: dairy

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 300g, cut into 2cm cubes Paneer
  • 1.5 cups (200g), fresh or frozen Peas
  • 2 medium (240g), roughly chopped Onion
  • 3 medium (300g), roughly chopped Tomato
  • 1.5 inch Ginger
  • 5 cloves Garlic
  • 2 Green Chilli
  • 3 tbsp Gheeor neutral oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 Bay Leafoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
  • 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp, crushed Kasuri Methi
  • 1/2 tsp Sugarbalances sharp tomatoesoptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 1.5 cups Water
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • If the paneer is firm from the fridge, soak the cubes in hot salted water 15 minutes and drain just before adding.
  • Blend the onion, tomato, ginger, garlic and chilli into a smooth paste and keep it ready — this gravy comes together fast.
  • Frozen peas go in at the end; fresh peas need 8 minutes of simmering.

Method

  1. Blend the onion, tomato, ginger, garlic and green chillies to a smooth paste with 2 tbsp water.
  2. Heat the ghee in a kadhai, add the bay leaf and cumin seeds, and let them crackle 20 seconds.
  3. Pour in the blended paste — stand back, it spits — and cook on medium for 15 minutes.Keep going until the paste darkens to brick red and oil clearly separates at the edges. Half-cooked paste tastes raw and metallic.
  4. Stir in the turmeric, coriander powder and kashmiri chilli and fry 1 minute.
  5. Add 1.5 cups water, 1 tsp salt and the sugar, and bring to a simmer.
  6. Add the peas and simmer 8 minutes for fresh, 4 minutes for frozen, until tender.
  7. Slide in the paneer cubes and simmer 4 minutes on low.Low heat only. Boiled paneer goes tough and squeaky.
  8. Crush in the kasuri methi and add the garam masala.
  9. Rest off the heat 5 minutes so the paneer takes up the gravy, then taste for salt.
  10. Scatter the coriander and serve with roti or rice.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat gently on the hob with a splash of water. Not suitable for freezing — the paneer turns spongy.
