Punjabi

Bread Pakora

bread stuffed with spiced potato, dipped in gram flour batter and fried

dairy freeegg freeno onion garlicnut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Active40 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: gluten

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 8 slices, a day old Breadday-old bread absorbs less oil
  • 400g, boiled and crushed Potato
  • 180g Besan
  • 2, chopped Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp, grated Ginger
  • 1 tsp Amchur
  • 1 tsp Chaat Masala
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • a handful Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 600ml Neutral Oilfor deep frying
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Use bread that has dried a little. Fresh soft bread drinks oil and collapses.
  • Press the sandwich firmly before battering so the filling does not escape into the pan.

Method

  1. Mix the crushed potato with chilli, ginger, amchur, chaat masala, coriander and salt.
  2. Spread thickly on four slices, top with the rest and press down hard. Cut each into two triangles.
  3. Whisk the besan with turmeric, chilli powder, salt and water into a batter thick enough to cling.Thicker than a pakora batter. It has to seal the cut edges.
  4. Heat the oil to 175C. Dip each triangle, making sure the cut sides are covered, and lower it in.
  5. Fry 3 minutes a side until deep gold and crisp.
  6. Drain, cut once more if you like, dust with chaat masala and serve with tamarind chutney.

Storage

Eat straight away. These go soggy faster than any other fried snack.
