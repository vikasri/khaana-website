Tamil Nadu

Seedai

small rice flour spheres fried crisp for Krishna Jayanthi

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep30 min
Cook25 min
Active55 min
Plus20 min drying
Serves30
Difficultyadvanced

Contains: milk, sesame

Ingredients

Quantities for 30.

  • 250g Rice Flour
  • 3 tbsp, roasted and ground Urad Dal
  • 30g Butter
  • 3 tbsp, grated Coconut
  • 1 tbsp Sesame
  • 1/2 tsp Asafoetida
  • as needed Water
  • 500ml for deep frying Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Seedai has a reputation for bursting in the oil. Two things cause it: air pockets in the balls and undissolved salt.
  • Dissolve the salt in the water before it touches the flour, and roll each ball smooth with no cracks.

Method

  1. Sieve the flours together. Dissolve the salt and asafoetida in a little water and add that, not dry salt.Undissolved salt crystals are the classic cause of a seedai exploding.
  2. Rub in the butter, coconut and sesame, then bring together into a stiff dough with water.
  3. Roll small marble-sized balls, keeping each one completely smooth with no cracks.A crack is where the steam gets in. Roll them properly.
  4. Spread the balls on a cloth and air-dry 20 minutes.
  5. Fry a small batch on medium first as a test. If it holds, fry the rest until the bubbling stops.Test batch first, and stand back from the pan for the first minute.
  6. Drain and cool completely.

Storage

Keeps 2 weeks in an airtight tin at room temperature. The coconut is what limits it; beyond that it can turn.
