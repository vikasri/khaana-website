Hyderabadi

Gil-e-Firdaus

bottle gourd cooked down in thickened milk with sago, a wedding-table pudding

egg freegluten freevegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook45 min
Active65 min
Plus3 hr chilling
Serves6
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, tree nuts

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 600g, peeled, deseeded and grated Bottle Gourdweigh after peeling; a young pale gourd is sweeter
  • 1.2 litres full fat Milk
  • 50g small pearls Sabudana
  • 200g Condensed Milk
  • 80g, to taste Sugar
  • 100g, grated Khoyaoptional
  • 6 green, seeds ground Cardamom
  • a good pinch, soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk Saffronoptional
  • 20, blanched and slivered Almonds
  • 20, slivered Pistachiosoptional
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 tsp Rose Wateroptional

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Soak the sabudana in cold water for 30 minutes, then drain. Skipping this leaves hard white centres in the finished pudding.
  • Squeeze the grated bottle gourd lightly and keep the juice; add it back with the milk rather than throwing away the flavour.
  • This is a slow reduction. Use the heaviest pan you own or the milk will catch and taste scorched all the way through.

Method

  1. Fry the grated bottle gourd in the ghee over medium heat 8 minutes, until it loses its raw smell and the volume halves.Cook off the water now. Wet gourd added to milk gives a thin, vegetal pudding.
  2. Pour in the milk and any reserved gourd juice, bring to a boil, then lower to a steady simmer.
  3. Simmer uncovered 25 minutes, stirring every few minutes and scraping the base and sides, until the milk has reduced by about a third.Scrape the bottom with a flat spatula, not a spoon. Milk solids settle in the corners and burn there first.
  4. Add the drained sabudana and cook 10 minutes, stirring often, until the pearls are translucent with no white dot in the centre.
  5. Stir in the condensed milk and sugar and cook 5 minutes, until dissolved and slightly thickened.
  6. Fold in the grated khoya and cook 3 minutes, until melted and the pudding coats the back of a spoon.
  7. Take off the heat and stir in the ground cardamom, saffron milk and rose water.Add the rose water off the heat. Boiled, its perfume disappears and only a faint soapiness stays.
  8. Stir in half the slivered almonds and pistachios and cool 20 minutes.
  9. Chill at least 3 hours, then serve cold in small bowls scattered with the remaining nuts. It thickens as it chills.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated in a covered container. Serve cold; it thickens further overnight, so loosen with 2-3 tbsp cold milk before serving. Do not freeze.
