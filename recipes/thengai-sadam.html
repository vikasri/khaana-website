Tamil Nadu

Thengai Sadam

leftover rice tossed through toasted coconut, dals and curry leaves

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freeno onion garlichealthier
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Total25 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: nuts

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 3 cups cooked and cooled Riceday-old rice, grains separate
  • 1 cup, fresh grated Coconut
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tbsp Urad Dal
  • 1 tbsp Chana Dal
  • 3, broken Dried Red Chilli
  • 2, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 12 Cashewoptional
  • 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 1 tsp, finely chopped Gingeroptional
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 3 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Cook the rice at least 2 hours ahead, spread it on a wide plate and let it cool completely. Warm rice turns this to mush.
  • Grate the coconut fresh. Desiccated coconut will not give the moisture this dish needs.

Method

  1. Spread the cooled rice on a wide plate, sprinkle with salt and 1 tbsp of the coconut oil, and separate the grains with your fingers.Oiling the rice before anything else is added keeps the grains from clumping later.
  2. Dry roast the grated coconut in a kadhai over low heat for 4 to 5 minutes until it just turns pale gold and smells toasted. Set aside.Stop while it is blonde. Brown coconut tastes of caramel and overwhelms the dish.
  3. Heat the remaining coconut oil in the same kadhai and crackle the mustard seeds.
  4. Add the urad dal, chana dal and cashews and fry until all three are golden, about 2 minutes.
  5. Add the red chillies, slit green chillies, ginger, curry leaves and asafoetida and fry 30 seconds until the leaves crisp.
  6. Return the toasted coconut to the pan and stir for 1 minute so it takes up the tempering.
  7. Turn off the heat, tip in the rice and fold everything together gently with a flat spatula.Fold, do not stir. A spoon breaks the grains and you lose the texture entirely.
  8. Cover and let it sit 5 minutes so the flavours settle, then check the salt and serve.

Storage

Keeps a full day at room temperature, which is why it travels so well, and 3 days refrigerated. Bring back to room temperature before eating rather than microwaving, which dries the coconut out.
