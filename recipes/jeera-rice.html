Punjabi

Jeera Rice

basmati tossed with cumin in ghee, the rice that goes with everything

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep5 min
Cook20 min
Active25 min
Plus20 min soaking
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 300g, rinsed and soaked 20 minutes Basmati Rice
  • 2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 3 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 Bay Leafoptional
  • 1 small piece Cinnamonoptional
  • 3 Clovesoptional
  • a little, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Soaking is not optional for basmati. Twenty minutes lets the grain absorb water evenly so it lengthens instead of breaking.
  • Drain the soaked rice properly. Wet rice going into hot ghee spits and steams.

Method

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then soak 20 minutes and drain well.
  2. Heat the ghee, add the whole spices and let the cumin darken by a shade and smell toasted.A shade darker, no more. Burnt cumin is bitter and cannot be rescued.
  3. Add the drained rice and turn it gently in the ghee for 2 minutes until every grain is coated.
  4. Pour in 540ml hot water, add salt, and bring to a hard boil.Hot water, not cold. Cold water stalls the pan and the grains cook unevenly.
  5. Cover, drop to the lowest heat and cook 12 minutes without lifting the lid.
  6. Rest 10 minutes off the heat, then fork through with the coriander.The rest is when the grains firm up. Forking too early breaks them.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat with a sprinkle of water under a lid, or fry in a little ghee. Cool leftover rice quickly and refrigerate within the hour.
