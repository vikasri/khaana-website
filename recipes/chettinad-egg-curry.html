Chettinad Muttai Masala

boiled eggs simmered in a roasted coconut and pepper masala

dairy freegluten freehealthiernut free
Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Active50 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: egg, sesame

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 6, hard-boiled and peeled Eggs
  • 12 small, finely chopped Shallot
  • 2 medium, chopped Tomato
  • 8 cloves Garlic
  • 1 inch piece Ginger
  • 1/2 cup, freshly grated Coconut
  • 6 Dried Red Chilli
  • 2 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 1.5 tsp whole Black Pepper
  • 1.5 tsp Fennel Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Stone Flowerkalpasi
  • 1 inch stick Cinnamon
  • 3 Cloves
  • 1 point broken off a whole star Star Anise
  • 2 tsp Poppy Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 3 tbsp Sesame Oilgingelly oil
  • to taste Salt
  • 350ml Water
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesto finishoptional

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai, blender

Before you start

  • Boil the eggs 9 minutes from cold water, then cool them under running water and peel. A full nine minutes gives a set but not chalky yolk.
  • Score each peeled egg lightly three times with a knife so the masala gets inside.
  • Roast and grind the masala before you start the gravy; it needs to cool fully before grinding.

Method

  1. Dry-roast the red chillies, coriander powder, pepper, fennel, cumin, poppy seeds, stone flower, cinnamon, cloves and star anise over low heat for 4 minutes.
  2. Add the coconut and roast 3 minutes more until it smells toasty and turns pale brown.Stop while the coconut is still light. Dark coconut makes the gravy taste burnt rather than deep.
  3. Cool completely and grind with 100ml water to a smooth paste.
  4. Heat 2 tbsp of the gingelly oil in a kadhai, add the scored eggs with 1/4 tsp turmeric and a pinch of salt, and fry 3 minutes turning until the skins are blistered and golden.
  5. Lift the eggs out and set aside; add the remaining oil and the curry leaves to the same pan.
  6. Fry the shallots for 6 minutes until golden, then add the ginger and garlic pounded to a paste and fry 2 minutes.
  7. Add the tomatoes and remaining turmeric and cook 5 minutes until pulpy and the oil shows at the edges.
  8. Stir in the ground masala and fry on low for 6 minutes until it darkens and the oil separates.
  9. Add 250ml water and salt, bring to a boil, then simmer 8 minutes until the gravy coats the back of a spoon.
  10. Slip the fried eggs in, spoon gravy over them and simmer 5 minutes on the lowest heat.Do not boil hard with the eggs in. The whites go rubbery. A bare tremble is enough.
  11. Rest 5 minutes, scatter the coriander and serve with rice or chapati.

Safe temperatures: Egg dishes are done at 71°C / 160°F; a whole egg when the yolk and white are both firm. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat gently on the stovetop; the eggs firm up on standing, so add a splash of water and warm slowly.
