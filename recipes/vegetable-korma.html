Vegetable Korma

mixed vegetables in a mild coconut and cashew gravy, southern style

egg freegluten freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Active50 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: tree nuts

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 150g, diced Carrot
  • 100g French Beans
  • 200g, diced Potato
  • 100g Peas
  • 150g Caulifloweroptional
  • 100g, fresh grated Coconut
  • 40g Cashew
  • 200ml Coconut Milk
  • 2, sliced Onion
  • 3, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp Ginger
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Fennel Seeds
  • 3 Cardamom
  • 1 piece Cinnamon
  • 3 Cloves
  • 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 3 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • The southern korma leans on coconut rather than cream, and on fennel rather than garam masala. It should taste sweet and aromatic, not rich.
  • Add the thick coconut milk off the heat. Boiled coconut milk separates into oil and grit.

Method

  1. Blend the grated coconut, cashews, fennel and a little water to a fine paste.Fine. Any grit here stays gritty in the finished dish.
  2. Fry the whole spices and curry leaves in coconut oil, then the onion until soft but not brown.Not brown. This gravy should stay pale.
  3. Add ginger, garlic and green chilli, then the vegetables and enough hot water to barely cover.
  4. Simmer covered until the vegetables are just tender, about 12 minutes.
  5. Stir in the coconut-cashew paste and cook 5 minutes until it thickens.
  6. Take off the heat, stir in the thick coconut milk, and check the salt.Off the heat. This is the step people get wrong.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat very gently; coconut milk splits on a hard boil. Not suitable for freezing.
