Bengali

Labra

a soft mixed vegetable torkari fragrant with panch phoron and ginger

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freenut freeno onion garlichealthier
Prep25 min
Cook35 min
Total60 min
Serves6
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 250g, in 3cm cubes Pumpkinthe sweetness that holds labra together
  • 2 medium, in 3cm cubes Potato
  • 1 medium, in 3cm cubes Sweet Potato
  • 1 small, in 3cm cubes Eggplant
  • 1, peeled and thickly sliced Raw Banana
  • 100g, in 3cm lengths French Beansoptional
  • 150g, roughly shredded Cabbageoptional
  • 1.5 tsp Panch Phoron
  • 2 Dried Red Chilli
  • 2 Bay Leaf
  • 1 tbsp, grated Ginger
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidafor a gluten-free dish use compounded-free hing — most brands are cut with wheat flour
  • 4 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
  • to taste Salt
  • 200ml Water

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Cut everything to roughly 3cm so the hard and soft vegetables finish at the same time.
  • Toast 1 tsp of the panch phoron in a dry pan and crush it — this goes in at the end as a finishing powder.
  • Labra wants to be stirred with a light hand; use a wide kadhai so you can fold rather than mash.

Method

  1. Heat the mustard oil to smoking in a wide kadhai, then reduce the heat.
  2. Add the bay leaves, dried red chillies and the remaining 1/2 tsp whole panch phoron. Let them crackle 15 seconds.The fenugreek in panch phoron turns bitter if it darkens past golden brown.
  3. Add the asafoetida and the grated ginger and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Tip in the potato and sweet potato and fry 4 minutes until the edges are translucent.
  5. Add the pumpkin, raw banana, beans and cabbage with the turmeric, cumin powder and salt. Fold gently for 3 minutes.
  6. Add the eggplant last, along with 200ml water. Cover and cook on low for 18-20 minutes.Do not stir while it steams. Shake the pan by the handle instead.
  7. Uncover: the pumpkin should have collapsed and be binding everything into a rough mash.That collapsing pumpkin is the texture you are after — if it still holds cubes, give it 5 more minutes covered.
  8. Cook uncovered 4-5 minutes to drive off the last of the water.
  9. Stir in the sugar, garam masala and the crushed toasted panch phoron. Rest 5 minutes and serve with khichuri or rice.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and tastes better on day two. Reheat in a pan with a splash of water; it does not freeze well.
