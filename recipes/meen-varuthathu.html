Kerala

Meen Varuthathu

chilli-and-pepper crusted fish shallow fried in coconut oil until the edges blister

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut freepescatarian
Prep15 min
Cook15 min
Total30 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: fish

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 600g, 4 steaks or whole small fish, slashed Fishseer, pomfret or mackerel
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri Chillifor colour without ferocious heat
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powderoptional
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp, coarsely ground Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp, paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp, paste Garlic
  • 1, juiced Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Rice Flourfor the crustoptional
  • 3 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 5 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, tawa

Before you start

  • Pat the fish bone dry with paper towel and cut two deep slashes into each side, right down to the bone. Wet fish will never crisp.
  • Mix the chilli powders, turmeric, pepper, ginger, garlic, lemon juice and salt into a thick paste with 1 tbsp water and rub it into the slashes.
  • Marinate 30 minutes in the refrigerator, or up to 2 hours. Longer than that and the lemon starts to cook the flesh.

Method

  1. Bring the marinated fish back to room temperature for 15 minutes if it has been in the fridge.
  2. Dust each piece lightly with rice flour on both sides and shake off the excess.A thin film only. A thick coat turns pasty in the pan.
  3. Heat the coconut oil in a heavy tawa over medium-high until a pinch of flour dropped in sizzles instantly.
  4. Slide in the fish, laying it away from you, and do not touch it for 4 minutes.Moving it early tears the crust off. Wait until it releases on its own.
  5. Turn once and fry the second side 3-4 minutes until the crust is deep brick red and the flesh at the slash is opaque.
  6. Throw the curry leaves into the hot oil around the fish for the last 30 seconds until they crisp.Stand back. Wet leaves in hot oil spit hard.
  7. Lift onto a plate, scatter the crisped leaves over, and serve within minutes with rice and a wedge of lemon.

Storage

Eat hot from the pan. Leftovers keep 1 day refrigerated; re-crisp in a dry hot pan for 2 minutes a side rather than reheating in a microwave.
