Beetroot Ginger Shot

earthy rather than sharp, and the one that stains

dairy freeegg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freeveganvegetarianwellness shot
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook0 min
Active10 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Allergens in Beetroot Ginger Shot: none of the ten listed below.

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 1 medium, peeled and cubed Beetroot
  • 20g fresh, peeled Ginger
  • 1, juiced Orange
  • ½, juiced Lemon
  • a pinch Black Saltoptional

Cookware

blender

Ingredient names in English →

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Method

  1. Blend the beetroot and ginger with 100ml water until completely smooth. A blender needs longer here than it looks like it should.
  2. Strain through a fine sieve or muslin, pressing hard. Beetroot pulp holds on to its juice.
  3. Stir in the orange and lemon juice and the black salt.
  4. Pour into two glasses.

Storage

Two days refrigerated. It will stain a plastic jug permanently; use glass.
