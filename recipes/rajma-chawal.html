Punjabi

Rajma Chawal

soft kidney beans in a thick onion-tomato gravy, eaten over plain rice

dairy freeegg freegluten freehealthiernut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep20 min
Cook60 min
Active80 min
Plus8 hr soaking
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Start the day before. The soaking alone is 8 hr, against 80 min of actual work.

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1.5 cups (300g), red kidney beans Rajmathe small dark Jammu variety if you can find it
  • 1.5 cups (300g) Basmati Rice
  • 2 large (300g), finely chopped Onion
  • 3 medium (300g), pureed Tomato
  • 1.5 inch, grated Ginger
  • 6 cloves, minced Garlic
  • 2, slit Green Chilli
  • 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 Bay Leafoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 3/4 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1/2 tsp Amchuror a squeeze of lemon at the endoptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 4 cups for the beans, 3 cups for the rice Water
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional

Cookware

stovetop, pressure cooker

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Soak the rajma in cold water overnight, 8 to 10 hours. A shorter soak means a longer and less even cook, not a bean that never comes good.
  • Rinse the basmati until the water runs clear, then soak it 20 minutes and drain.
  • Discard the bean soaking water and cook in fresh water. It makes the dish easier on the stomach.

Method

  1. Pressure cook the drained rajma with 4 cups fresh water, the bay leaf and 1 tsp salt for 20 minutes at full pressure, then let the pressure fall naturally.A cooked bean should squash to a paste between finger and thumb with no resistance.
  2. While it cooks, heat the oil in a heavy pan and crackle the cumin seeds for 20 seconds.
  3. Add the onion and fry on medium-high, stirring often, until evenly deep brown. A full 12 to 14 minutes.Pale onions give a thin, pink gravy. Take it to the colour of strong tea.
  4. Stir in the ginger, garlic and green chillies and fry 2 minutes until the sharpness goes.
  5. Add the turmeric, coriander powder and chilli powder, stir for 20 seconds, then pour in the tomato puree.Adding the tomato quickly stops the ground spices burning in the dry pan.
  6. Cook the masala down for 10 to 12 minutes until it darkens and oil separates at the sides.
  7. Scoop out a ladle of cooked beans, mash them to a paste, and stir that back in with the rest of the beans and their liquid.The mashed beans are what makes the gravy cling to rice instead of running off it.
  8. Simmer uncovered 20 minutes until the gravy is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. Add hot water if it tightens too far.
  9. Meanwhile boil the rice in 3 cups water with 1/2 tsp salt, covered, on the lowest heat for 11 minutes, then rest 5 minutes off the heat and fork through.
  10. Finish the rajma with garam masala and amchur, taste for salt, and scatter over the coriander.
  11. Serve the rajma ladled over the hot rice.

Storage

The rajma keeps 4 days refrigerated and improves overnight; reheat with a splash of water. Cook rice fresh. It goes hard in the fridge. The gravy freezes well for 2 months.
