Wellness

Haldi Adrak Shot

turmeric and ginger with the black pepper that makes it work

dairy freeegg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarianwellness shot
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep5 min
Cook0 min
Active5 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 30g fresh, peeled Turmeric2 tsp ground will do; the colour stains everything it meets
  • 30g fresh, peeled Ginger
  • 1, juiced Lemon
  • a good pinch, coarsely cracked Black Peppernot optional, see below
  • 1 tsp Honeyoptional

Cookware

blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Method

  1. Blend the turmeric, ginger and 80ml water to a slurry.
  2. Strain hard through a fine sieve or a muslin. Squeeze the pulp dry; most of the yield is in the last press.
  3. Stir in the lemon juice, black pepper and honey.
  4. Divide into two small glasses and drink.

Storage

Keeps three days in a sealed jar in the fridge. Shake before pouring; it separates.
