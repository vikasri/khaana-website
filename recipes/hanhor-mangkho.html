Northeast Indian

Hanhor Mangkho

assamese duck stewed slowly with ash gourd, peppery and lean rather than creamy

gluten freedairy freenut freehealthier
Prep20 min
Cook75 min
Total95 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1kg, on the bone, cut into curry pieces Duck
  • 500g, peeled and cut into 4cm chunks Ash Gourdthe traditional partner; it drinks up the fat
  • 3 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 2 medium, finely sliced Onion
  • 2 tbsp, ground to a paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp, ground to a paste Garlic
  • 2 tsp, coarsely crushed Black Pepperpepper carries this curry, not chilli powder — crush it fresh
  • 2 Bay Leaf
  • 5cm stick Cinnamon
  • 4 Cloves
  • 2, bruised Black Cardamomoptional
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 3, slit Green Chillioptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 600ml, hot Water

Equipment

stovetop

Before you start

  • Duck carries a lot of fat under the skin. Dry-fry the pieces first with no oil for a few minutes and pour off what renders, or the curry sits under a slick.
  • Grind the ginger and garlic fresh into a coarse paste — a duck this strongly flavoured flattens shop-bought paste.
  • Ash gourd goes in only for the last stretch. Added early it dissolves completely.

Method

  1. Put the duck pieces in a dry heavy pot over medium heat and cook 6 minutes, turning, until fat pools in the base. Pour that fat off and set the duck aside.This one step is the difference between a clean curry and a greasy one.
  2. Heat the mustard oil in the same pot until it smokes, cool it a minute, then fry the bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves and black cardamom for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced onion and cook 12 minutes over medium heat, until deep gold and shrunk to a third of its volume.Do not rush this — pale onion leaves the curry thin and sweetish.
  4. Stir in the ginger and garlic paste and fry 3 minutes, until the raw smell goes and the oil starts to separate at the edges.
  5. Add the turmeric and crushed black pepper, stir for 20 seconds, then return the duck and toss to coat every piece.
  6. Fry the duck in the masala 8 minutes, scraping the base as it catches, until the pieces are browned all over.
  7. Pour in the hot water, add salt, cover and simmer very gently 45 minutes, until a skewer goes into the thigh with no resistance.
  8. Add the ash gourd and green chillies and simmer uncovered 15 minutes more, until the gourd is translucent and the gravy has reduced to a coating consistency.Translucent, not collapsed — check at 10 minutes, it can go from firm to mush quickly.
  9. Rest 10 minutes off the heat before serving with plain rice.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and is better on the second day. Skim the set fat from the top before reheating, then warm gently on the stovetop with a few spoons of water.
