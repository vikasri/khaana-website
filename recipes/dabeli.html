Dabeli

a spiced potato bun from Kutch, pressed on a griddle and edged with sev and pomegranate

egg freevegetarian
Prep25 min
Cook20 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, gluten, peanuts

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 8 rolls Pav
  • 500g, boiled and mashed Potato
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powderwith the cinnamon and clove below, this is dabeli masala
  • 1.5 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground Cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground Cloves
  • 4 tbsp sweet extract Tamarind
  • 2 tbsp Jaggery
  • 80g, roasted Peanut
  • 4 tbsp seeds Pomegranate
  • 1, finely chopped Onion
  • 60g Besanfor sev
  • 4 tbsp Butter
  • a handful Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 400ml Neutral Oilfor frying the sev
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, tawa

Before you start

  • Dabeli means pressed, and that is the technique: the filled bun is pressed hard on a buttered griddle until the outside crisps.
  • The filling should be sweet, sour and hot at once. If it tastes of only one of those, it is not finished.

Method

  1. Mix the ground spices with the tamarind and jaggery into a thick paste.
  2. Fold this through the mashed potato with salt until evenly coloured, and spread it flat on a plate.
  3. Press pomegranate seeds, roasted peanuts and coriander over the surface.Pressed into the surface, not stirred through. You want them in every bite of the cut face.
  4. Slit the pav without cutting through, spread a little tamarind chutney inside, and fill generously.
  5. Roll the open edges in sev so the filling face is crusted.
  6. Griddle in butter, pressing down firmly with a spatula, until both faces are crisp and golden.
  7. Serve hot with more sev, chopped onion and roasted peanuts alongside.

Storage

Best hot off the griddle. The filling keeps 2 days refrigerated and is arguably better on day two; assemble to order.
