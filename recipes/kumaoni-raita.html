Pahari

Kumaoni Raita

grated cucumber in yogurt sharpened with ground mustard until it stings the nose

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep15 min
Cook5 min
Active20 min
Plus20 min chilling
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk, mustard

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g, whisked smooth Yogurtthick set curd, not runny
  • 2 medium, peeled and coarsely grated Cucumber
  • 2 tsp Mustard Seedsyellow or brown; this is the defining flavour
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 2 Green Chilli
  • 1/2 tsp Chilli Powderfor dusting on topoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Black Saltoptional
  • 1 tsp Mustard Oilfor the temperingoptional
  • 1, broken Dried Red Chillioptional
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 2 tbsp Waterfor grinding the mustard
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Grate the cucumber and squeeze out the liquid 15 minutes before you mix, or the raita will turn watery within the hour.
  • Grind the mustard seeds fresh with a little water. Pre-ground mustard powder has lost the volatile heat that gives this raita its kick.
  • Take the yogurt out of the fridge 20 minutes ahead; fridge-cold yogurt mutes the mustard.

Method

  1. Soak the mustard seeds in the 2 tbsp water for 10 minutes, then grind with the green chillies and turmeric to a smooth, bright yellow paste in a blender.The paste should smell so sharp it makes your eyes water. If it does not, grind another minute.
  2. Squeeze the grated cucumber firmly in your fist over the sink and discard the liquid.
  3. Whisk the yogurt in a bowl until completely smooth with no lumps, about 1 minute.
  4. Fold in the mustard paste a teaspoon at a time, tasting as you go, until the raita is as pungent as you want it.Add it gradually. Too much ground mustard turns bitter and there is no way back.
  5. Stir in the squeezed cucumber, the salt and the black salt.
  6. Heat the mustard oil in a small pan until it smokes, drop in the broken red chilli for 10 seconds, and pour the tempering over the raita.
  7. Chill for at least 20 minutes so the mustard heat settles into the yogurt.Straight after mixing the mustard sits on top as a raw burn; 20 minutes rounds it out.
  8. Dust with chilli powder, scatter the coriander leaves and serve cold with aloo ke gutke or rice.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated in a covered box, though the mustard heat fades after the first day. Do not freeze; the yogurt splits. Stir before serving.
