Northeast Indian

Kinema

fermented soybeans cooked with tomato and chilli, pungent and deeply savoury

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut freeveganvegetarian
Prep15 min
Cook25 min
Active40 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: mustard, soy

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 250g, crumbled and left uncovered overnight Tofustanding in for kinema, fermented whole soybeans; the flavour is milder but the base is right
  • 3, chopped Tomato
  • 2, chopped Onion
  • 8 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Ginger
  • 5 Green Chilli
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • a handful Coriander Leaves
  • 3 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Kinema is a Limbu and Nepali ferment of the eastern Himalaya, close kin to Japanese natto. It smells strong and tastes savoury rather than sour.
  • Real kinema cannot be improvised. This uses crumbled tofu for texture and leans on the mustard oil and chilli for character; it is a version, not the thing itself.

Method

  1. Fry the crumbled tofu in the mustard oil until browned and dry at the edges, 8 minutes.
  2. Add the onion, garlic, ginger and green chillies and cook until soft.
  3. Add the turmeric and tomato and cook down until the oil separates, about 8 minutes.
  4. Add 150ml water and simmer 5 minutes into a thick, spoonable curry.
  5. Season and finish with coriander. Serve with rice.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated. Reheat to a full simmer.
