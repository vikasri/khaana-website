Karnataka

Kadambuttu

soft steamed rice dumplings, the standing partner to pandi curry

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freenut free
Prep25 min
Cook30 min
Total55 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 2 cups Rice Flourcoarse rice rava is traditional; fine rice flour gives a smoother, slightly softer dumpling
  • 3.5 cups Water
  • 1/3 cup fresh grated Coconutfor fragrance and a little biteoptional
  • 2 tsp Coconut Oil
  • 1 tsp Salt

Cookware

stovetop, steamer

Before you start

  • Boil the water in a wide, heavy pan. A thin pan scorches the flour the moment it hits the base.
  • Shape the dumplings while the dough is still hot enough to be uncomfortable to hold. Cooled dough cracks and will not seal.
  • Keep a bowl of cold water beside you for dipping your palms between dumplings.
  • Have the steamer already at a rolling boil before the first dumpling is rolled.

Method

  1. Bring the water to a full boil with the salt and coconut oil in a wide heavy pan.
  2. Lower the heat to minimum and add the rice flour all at once, stirring hard with a wooden spoon.Add it in one go, not in stages. Sprinkling flour in slowly is how you get lumps.
  3. Keep stirring 2 to 3 minutes until the mixture pulls away from the sides in a single rough mass with no dry flour left.
  4. Cover the pan and leave it on the lowest heat for 5 minutes so the flour steams through.
  5. Tip the dough onto a plate, scatter over the grated coconut and let it cool just enough to handle, about 3 minutes.
  6. Knead the hot dough with wet hands for 3 minutes until it is completely smooth and slightly shiny.This kneading is the whole recipe. Under-kneaded dough gives dumplings that crack open in the steamer.
  7. Break off lime-sized pieces and roll each between wet palms into a smooth ball with no seams or cracks.
  8. Arrange the balls in a greased steamer plate, leaving a finger's gap between them.
  9. Steam over rapidly boiling water for 12 minutes, until the surfaces look matt and set rather than wet.
  10. Turn off the heat and leave them in the covered steamer 5 minutes before lifting them out.Straight out of the steam they are sticky; the short rest firms them up.

Storage

Best within a few hours. Keeps 2 days refrigerated in a covered box; re-steam 5 minutes to soften. They go hard and chalky in the fridge if left uncovered.
