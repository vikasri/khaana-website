Awadhi/Lucknowi

Palak Shorba

Spinach wilted and blended while still green, sharp with garlic and pepper

egg freegluten freenut freesoupvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook20 min
Active30 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 400g, washed Spinach
  • 1 small, chopped Onion
  • 4 cloves, chopped Garlic
  • 2 tsp, chopped Ginger
  • 1, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 tsp, coarsely cracked Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Butteror oil, which makes it vegan
  • 100ml Milkoptional; softens the iron edgeoptional
  • 1/2 Lemonoptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 700ml Water

Cookware

stovetop, blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Everything here is about keeping it green. Spinach turns from bright to army khaki in about four minutes of boiling, and no amount of cream brings it back.
  • Have the blender ready before the spinach goes in. This is a soup that is cooked in the order of a stir-fry.

Method

  1. Warm the butter and crackle the cumin, then add the onion, garlic, ginger and chilli and cook 4 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the water and salt and bring to a boil.
  3. Drop in the spinach, push it under, and cook 2 minutes at most — until it has collapsed and no longer.Two minutes. This is the step the dish lives or dies on.
  4. Take it off the heat immediately and blend, then pass through a sieve.Blending it hot off the heat rather than after it has sat keeps the colour; so does not putting the lid on afterwards.
  5. Return to the pan, add the cracked pepper and the milk if using, and bring back to just below a simmer. Do not boil it again.
  6. Check the salt, sharpen with lemon, and serve at once.At once. It is at its best within ten minutes and merely good after thirty.

Storage

Best the day it is made — it dulls in colour overnight, though it still tastes right. Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently and never boil.
