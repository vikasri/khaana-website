Koli Saaru

peppery kannadiga chicken curry in a roasted coconut and poppy seed gravy

gluten freedairy freenut freehealthier
Prep25 min
Cook45 min
Total70 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 800g, bone-in, cut into curry pieces Chickenbone-in matters for the saaru
  • 2 large, 1 sliced and 1 finely chopped Onion
  • 2 medium, chopped Tomato
  • 3/4 cup, freshly grated Coconut
  • 1 tbsp Poppy Seeds
  • 8 byadgi Dried Red Chilli
  • 1 tsp, whole Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 2cm piece Cinnamon
  • 4 Cloves
  • 10 cloves Garlic
  • 3cm piece Ginger
  • 3/4 tsp Turmeric
  • 15 Curry Leaves
  • 4 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • 1/4 cup, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 1/2, juiced Lemonto finishoptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 cups Water

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai, blender

Before you start

  • Rub the chicken with 1/2 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp salt and leave it 30 minutes at room temperature while you make the masala.
  • Roast and grind the coconut masala before you start cooking — it cannot be rushed once the chicken is in the pan.
  • Use fresh coconut if you can. Desiccated gives a thinner, sweeter gravy.

Method

  1. Heat 1 tbsp of the coconut oil and fry the sliced onion 6 minutes until browned at the edges. Set aside.
  2. In the same pan roast the red chillies, black pepper, cumin, cinnamon and cloves 1 minute until they smell sharp, then add the poppy seeds for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the grated coconut and roast 4 minutes over medium-low, stirring constantly, until it turns an even golden brown and smells toasted.Constant stirring, medium-low heat. Coconut scorches in patches and one burnt patch makes the whole saaru bitter.
  4. Cool, then grind the roasted spices, coconut, browned onion, garlic, ginger and coriander powder with 3/4 cup water to a very smooth dark paste.
  5. Heat the remaining 3 tbsp coconut oil in a kadhai, add the curry leaves and the chopped onion and fry 8 minutes until soft and deep gold.
  6. Add the tomato and remaining turmeric and cook 5 minutes until the tomato collapses and oil beads at the edges.
  7. Add the chicken and fry on high 6 minutes, turning the pieces, until they are opaque all over and lightly seared.Sear in two batches if the pan is crowded — chicken that stews instead of searing makes a flat saaru.
  8. Stir in the ground masala and cook 6 minutes on medium, scraping the base, until it darkens by a shade and the oil separates.
  9. Pour in 1 1/4 cups hot water and salt to taste, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer 20 minutes on low until the chicken is falling off the bone and the gravy is the thickness of pouring cream.
  10. Finish with the lemon juice and coriander leaves, rest 10 minutes off the heat and serve with ragi mudde or akki rotti.The rest lets the gravy thicken and the pepper come forward.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and improves overnight. Reheat gently on the stovetop with a splash of water; do not boil hard or the coconut gravy will split.
