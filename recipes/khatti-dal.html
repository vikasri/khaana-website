Hyderabadi

Khatti Dal

everyday toor dal made unapologetically sour with tamarind

dairy freeegg freegluten freehealthiernut freeveganvegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook35 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: mustard

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 200g, rinsed until the water runs clear Toor Dal
  • 30g pulp, soaked in 150ml hot water Tamarind
  • 2, chopped Tomato
  • 4, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 small, finely chopped Onionoptional
  • 6 cloves, lightly crushed Garlic
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1/4 tsp Fenugreek Seedsoptional
  • 3, broken Dried Red Chilli
  • 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause a compounded-free asafoetida to keep the dish gluten-free
  • 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 800ml Water
  • to taste Salt
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional

Cookware

stovetop, pressure cooker

Before you start

  • Soak the toor dal 20 minutes if you have the time. It cooks softer and faster.
  • Soak the tamarind in hot water for 20 minutes, squeeze it out with your fingers and strain; the dal should be assertively sour, not merely tangy.
  • Most block asafoetida is cut with wheat flour, so buy a compounded-free one if gluten matters to you.

Method

  1. Pressure cook the dal with the turmeric, chopped tomato, green chillies and 700ml water for 4 whistles, about 12 minutes.
  2. Let the pressure fall on its own, then whisk the dal hard for 30 seconds until it is smooth and creamy with no whole lentils left.Whisking, not blending. A blender makes it gluey.
  3. Return the pan to a low flame, add the remaining water and salt, and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Stir in the tamarind water and simmer 8 minutes, until the raw sour smell has cooked off and the dal has thickened slightly.Taste after these 8 minutes, not before. Tamarind mellows as it cooks.
  5. Heat the oil in a small pan for the tempering. Crackle the mustard seeds, then the cumin and fenugreek.
  6. Add the crushed garlic and fry until it turns golden, about 1 minute, then the dried red chillies, curry leaves and asafoetida. It will spit hard.Take the pan off the heat before the asafoetida goes in. It scorches in seconds.
  7. If using onion, add it to the tempering and fry 3 minutes until soft and translucent.
  8. Pour the tempering into the dal, cover immediately for a minute to trap the aroma, then stir through.
  9. Finish with coriander leaves and serve with plain rice and a wedge of lime.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated. It sets firm when cold. Reheat with 100ml water per portion, stirring until it flows again, and refresh with a small second tempering if it tastes flat.
