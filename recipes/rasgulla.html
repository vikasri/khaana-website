Bengali

Rasgulla

Chhena dumplings boiled in light syrup until they double and turn spongy

vegetariangluten freenut free
Prep30 min
Cook25 min
Total55 min
Serves4
Difficultyadvanced

Contains: dairy

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 2 litres full fat Milknot UHT. It will not curdle cleanly
  • 2, juiced Lemonor 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 cups Sugar
  • 3, crushed Cardamomoptional
  • 1 tsp Rose Wateroptional
  • 1 tsp Maidaomit to keep it gluten-free; handle the balls gentlyoptional
  • 5 cups Water

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Everything depends on the chhena: drained enough to hold, moist enough to knead smooth. Squeeze it dry and the rasgulla turns rubbery.
  • The syrup must be thin. A thick syrup cannot penetrate the dumpling and it stays raw in the middle.

Method

  1. Bring the milk to a boil, take it off the heat, and add lemon juice a little at a time until the curds separate cleanly from greenish whey.
  2. Strain through muslin and rinse under cold water to wash out the acid. Hang 30 minutes, no longer.Rinsing matters; unrinsed chhena tastes of lemon.
  3. Knead the chhena on a plate with the heel of your hand for 8-10 minutes, until it goes from grainy to smooth and slightly greasy. Work in the maida if using.This is the step people cut short, and it is why rasgullas crack.
  4. Roll into 12 crack-free balls, smaller than you want them finished, they double.
  5. Bring the sugar and water to a rolling boil with the cardamom. Slide the balls in, cover, and boil hard for 15 minutes.Hard boil, lid on. A gentle simmer will not inflate them.
  6. Cool in the syrup, add rose water, and chill at least 4 hours before serving.

Storage

Keeps 4 days refrigerated in its syrup, and the texture is best from the second day. Do not freeze.
