Kashmiri

Harissa

mutton pounded with rice into a smooth paste, eaten at dawn in a Srinagar winter

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut free
Prep30 min
Cook300 min
Total330 min
Serves6
Difficultyadvanced

Contains: mustard

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 1kg, boneless shoulder Mutton
  • 150g, soaked Ricecooked down with the meat until neither is distinguishable
  • 2 tbsp Fennel Powder
  • 1 tbsp Dry Ginger Powder
  • 3 Black Cardamom
  • 1 stick Cinnamon
  • 5 Cloves
  • 8, fried crisp to finish Shallot
  • 150ml Mustard Oil
  • 2 litres Water
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Nothing to do with the North African chilli paste of the same name. This is a Kashmiri winter breakfast, sold from specialist shops that make nothing else.
  • It is pounded, not blended, for five or more hours until it is a smooth paste with no fibre left.

Method

  1. Boil the mutton with the whole spices, fennel, dry ginger, salt and the water. Skim carefully.
  2. Simmer 3 hours until the meat collapses at a touch. Fish out the whole spices.
  3. Add the soaked rice and cook a further hour until it has disintegrated.
  4. Now pound. Work it against the side of the pot with a heavy wooden spoon for at least an hour, until no fibre remains.This is the dish. Blending gives you a puree, which is a different and lesser thing.
  5. Adjust with hot water to a thick, pourable paste and check the salt.
  6. Serve in bowls with a ladle of smoking mustard oil and the crisp fried shallots over the top.The hot oil poured over at the table is part of it.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and sets solid when cold. Reheat with hot water, stirring hard to bring it back. Freezes for a month.
