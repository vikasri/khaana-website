Hyderabadi

Hyderabadi Khichdi

loose rice and moong dal cooked with whole spices, the city's breakfast plate

egg freegluten freehealthiernut freevegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Active35 min
Plus20 min soaking
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 200g short or medium grain, rinsed till the water runs clear Rice
  • 100g yellow, rinsed Moong Dal
  • 1 large, finely sliced Onion
  • 3, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp, julienned Ginger
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 3 green Cardamom
  • 4 Cloves
  • 2cm piece Cinnamon
  • 1 Bay Leafoptional
  • 1/2 tsp, whole Black Pepperoptional
  • 1/4 tsp Turmericoptional
  • 4 tbsp Gheethe dish is defined by it; oil gives a flatter result
  • 900ml Water
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, pressure cooker

Before you start

  • Soak the rice and dal together for 20 minutes, then drain well. This is what gives the grains their soft, separate texture.
  • Hyderabadi khichdi is eaten with khatta and kheema, so it is deliberately mild. Resist the urge to load it with spice powders.
  • Measure the water precisely: this khichdi should be loose and grainy, not a porridge.

Method

  1. Heat the ghee in a pressure cooker and crackle the cumin, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, peppercorns and bay leaf for 40 seconds.
  2. Add the sliced onion and fry over medium heat 8 minutes, until light golden.Stop at light gold, not brown. Dark onion muddies the pale colour this dish is supposed to have.
  3. Add the ginger and green chillies and fry 1 minute.
  4. Stir in the drained rice and dal and fry 3 minutes, until the grains look glossy and smell nutty.Frying the grains in ghee before the water goes in is what keeps them separate rather than sticky.
  5. Add the turmeric, salt and 900ml water, and stir once.
  6. Close the cooker and cook on medium for 2 whistles, then turn off the heat and let the pressure release naturally, about 10 minutes.
  7. Open up and fluff with a fork. The grains should be soft and just holding their shape, with no free liquid at the bottom.
  8. Rest covered 5 minutes, then serve hot with khichdi ka khatta and a spoon of extra ghee.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. It firms up cold; reheat with 3-4 tbsp hot water in a covered pan over low heat, breaking up lumps with a fork.
