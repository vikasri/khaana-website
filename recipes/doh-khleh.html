Northeast Indian

Doh Khleh

khasi pork salad. Boiled meat tossed hot with onion, chilli and a lot of lemon

gluten freedairy freenut freehealthier
Prep15 min
Cook40 min
Total55 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g belly or shoulder, in one piece Pork
  • 2 medium, finely sliced Onion
  • 3, finely chopped Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp, finely chopped Ginger
  • 2, juiced Lemondoh khleh should be properly sharp
  • 1 tsp, coarsely crushed Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp, roasted and coarsely ground Sesameblack sesame is what is used in Meghalaya; the catalogue's sesame is the same seed, so roast it a shade darkeroptional
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 1 Bay Leaffor the boiling wateroptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 1.5 litres for boiling Water

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Boil the pork in one piece and dice it after. Diced first, it dries out and loses its bounce.
  • Slice the onion paper-thin and salt it while the pork boils. Twenty minutes of sitting takes the harshness out.
  • Dress the salad while the pork is still warm. Cold meat will not take up the lemon.

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil with the bay leaf and a generous tablespoon of salt, lower in the pork and simmer 35 minutes.
  2. Test it with a skewer. It should go in easily but the meat should still hold together, not shred.Boil it past this point and the salad turns to threads.
  3. Meanwhile toss the sliced onion with a big pinch of salt and leave it in a bowl for 20 minutes.
  4. Roast the sesame in a dry pan 2 minutes, until it pops and smells toasted, then grind it coarsely.
  5. Lift the pork out, rest it 5 minutes, then cut it into 1.5cm dice, fat and all.
  6. While it is still warm, toss the pork with the onion and any liquid in its bowl, the ginger, green chilli and crushed black pepper.
  7. Add the lemon juice and check the salt. The boiling water has seasoned the meat already, so taste before adding more.Add the lemon to warm meat, not hot: straight off the boil it just evaporates.
  8. Fold in the ground sesame and coriander and serve at room temperature, on its own or with plain rice.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated but the onion softens and the lemon fades. Bring it to room temperature and refresh with a squeeze of lemon and fresh coriander; do not reheat.
