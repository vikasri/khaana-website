Bengali

Chicken Kathi Roll

the Calcutta original: egg paratha rolled around spiced chicken and raw onion

nut free
Prep30 min
Cook35 min
Active65 min
Plus2 hr marinating
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, gluten, egg

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 300g Maida
  • 500g boneless thigh, in strips Chicken
  • 4 Eggs
  • 150g Yogurt
  • 3, half for cooking and half sliced raw Onion
  • 1 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 2 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1.5 tsp Chaat Masala
  • 3, chopped Green Chilli
  • 2 Lemon
  • 6 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

tawa, stovetop

Before you start

  • The roll was invented for eating on the move, so it is built to hold: egg layer, dry filling, tight wrap.
  • Keep the chicken dry. A wet filling turns the paratha to pulp before you finish it.

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken strips in yogurt, ginger, garlic, chilli powder, turmeric and salt for at least 2 hours.
  2. Make a soft maida dough with oil and water; rest 30 minutes.
  3. Cook the chicken over high heat until the liquid has gone completely and the edges char, about 10 minutes.Cook it dry. This is the single most important step.
  4. Roll and griddle the parathas until just cooked, keeping them soft.
  5. Beat an egg, pour onto the tawa, press a paratha onto it, and flip after a minute.
  6. Lay chicken along the edge with plenty of raw onion, green chilli, chaat masala and a hard squeeze of lemon.
  7. Roll tight, wrap in paper and serve at once.

Safe temperatures: Chicken and duck are done at 74°C / 165°F, taken at the thickest part and clear of the bone. Egg dishes are done at 71°C / 160°F; a whole egg when the yolk and white are both firm. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Eat immediately. Cooked filling keeps 2 days refrigerated; dough keeps a day. Never assemble ahead.
