Northeast Indian

Chhurpi Soup

fermented cheese simmered with greens, the Sikkimese winter bowl

egg freegluten freenut freevegetarian
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 200g, cut small and dried hard Paneerstanding in for soft chhurpi, the fermented yak cheese
  • 200g, chopped Spinach
  • 100g, sliced Bamboo Shoot
  • 1, chopped Onion
  • 6 cloves, chopped Garlic
  • 1 tbsp, chopped Ginger
  • 3, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 1 litre Water
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Chhurpi is yak or cow milk cheese, fermented and either eaten soft or dried into a block hard enough to chew for hours.
  • Paneer is the honest substitute available here. It gives the texture but not the sourness, so the bamboo shoot is doing more work than usual.

Method

  1. Fry the onion, garlic and ginger in the oil until soft.
  2. Add the bamboo shoot and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add the water, bring to a boil and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Add the cheese and the green chillies and simmer 8 minutes until it softens and begins to melt at the edges.
  5. Stir in the spinach, cook 3 minutes, and season heavily with pepper and salt.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. The cheese firms up again when cold; reheat gently to a simmer rather than boiling.
