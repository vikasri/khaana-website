Bengali

Kolkata Biryani

pale, lightly spiced biryani with a whole potato and a boiled egg

gluten freehealthiernut free
Prep30 min
Cook50 min
Total80 min
Serves6
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, egg

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 800g, on the bone Mutton
  • 500g, soaked 30 minutes Basmati Rice
  • 6 medium, peeled and left whole Potatothe potato is not a filler here, it is the point
  • 6, hard-boiled Eggsoptional
  • 200g Yogurt
  • 3, fried brown Onion
  • 2 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 6 Cardamom
  • 2 pieces Cinnamon
  • 6 Cloves
  • 1/2 tsp Mace
  • a scraping Nutmeg
  • 1 tsp Kewra Waterthe perfume that marks a Kolkata biryani
  • a pinch in warm milk Saffron
  • 1/2 tsp Turmericfor the potatoes
  • 5 tbsp Ghee
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • This is the quiet biryani. Very little chilli, no tomato, and the spicing leans on mace, nutmeg and kewra rather than heat.
  • The potato is fried in turmeric first so it takes colour and holds its shape through the dum.

Method

  1. Rub the whole potatoes with turmeric and salt and fry in ghee until golden all over. Set aside.
  2. Fry the onions brown, drain, and reserve.
  3. Marinate the mutton in yogurt, ginger, garlic, mace, nutmeg, half the birista and salt for 4 hours.
  4. Cook the marinated mutton gently, covered, for 40 minutes until nearly tender, then add the fried potatoes.Unlike Hyderabadi kachchi, the meat here is cooked before layering.
  5. Boil the rice with the whole spices until 70 per cent done and drain.
  6. Layer rice over meat and potato, add the boiled eggs, and top with birista, saffron milk, kewra water and ghee.
  7. Seal and cook on the lowest heat 25 minutes, then rest 15 minutes before opening.Kewra goes in before the seal, not after. It needs the steam to carry it through the rice.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. The potato is best on the day; it goes grainy after. Reheat covered with a splash of water.
