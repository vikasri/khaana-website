Punjabi

Naan

soft leavened flatbread, blistered on top and chewy underneath

egg freenut freevegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Total40 min
Serves6
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, gluten

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 400g Maidaplain flour, not bread flour: naan should be soft, not chewy
  • 120g Yogurt
  • 150ml, warm Milk
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Soda
  • 1 tsp Enoor 1 tsp baking powderoptional
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 3 tbsp, for brushing Ghee
  • 1 tsp Nigellaoptional
  • 1 tsp Salt

Cookware

oven, tawa

Before you start

  • Naan needs a very hot surface and a soft, slack dough. Both matter more than the exact flour.
  • The dough should be softer than you think is sensible, close to sticky. A stiff dough gives a biscuit.

Method

  1. Mix the flour, salt, sugar, baking soda and eno. Work in the yogurt and enough warm milk to make a soft, slightly sticky dough.
  2. Knead 8 minutes until smooth, oil the surface, cover and rest 2 hours in a warm place.The rest does the work here, not the kneading.
  3. Heat a tawa until a drop of water skitters across it, or the oven with a tray inside at maximum.
  4. Divide into 6, roll each into a teardrop about 5mm thick, and scatter nigella on top.
  5. Wet the underside with a little water and slap it onto the hot tawa, wet side down. It will stick.The water is what makes it grip the surface, exactly like a tandoor wall. Do not skip it.
  6. When bubbles rise and the top sets, invert the pan over a flame to blister the top, 30 seconds.
  7. Brush with ghee and stack under a cloth so they stay soft.

Storage

Best within the hour. They keep a day wrapped in a cloth, and reheat well sprinkled with water in a hot pan. Freeze cooked with baking paper between them.
