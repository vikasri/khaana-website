Punjabi

Boondi Raita

gram flour pearls soaked soft in spiced yogurt

egg freegluten freenut freevegetarian
Prep15 min
Cook15 min
Active30 min
Plus20 min chilling
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 120g Besan
  • 500g, whisked smooth Yogurt
  • 1.5 tsp, roasted Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Black Salt
  • 1/2 tsp Chilli Powderoptional
  • a handful Coriander Leavesoptional
  • a few leaves Mintoptional
  • 400ml Neutral Oilfor frying the boondi
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Boondi must be soaked in warm water and squeezed before it goes into the yogurt. Dropped in dry, it stays hard and the raita turns to paste around it.
  • The batter should pour off a spoon in a thin stream. Thick batter gives tails instead of round pearls.

Method

  1. Whisk the besan with water and a pinch of salt into a smooth, thin, pouring batter.
  2. Hold a perforated spoon above hot oil at 180C and pour the batter through it so droplets fall in.Hold the spoon high enough that the drops round off on the way down.
  3. Fry 1 minute until pale and crisp, drain, and cool.
  4. Soak the boondi in warm water for 5 minutes, then squeeze gently between your palms.This is the step almost everyone skips, and it is the difference between good raita and gritty yogurt.
  5. Whisk the yogurt with salt, black salt and roasted cumin until pourable.
  6. Fold in the squeezed boondi, chill 20 minutes, and finish with chilli powder and herbs.

Storage

Best within a few hours; the boondi keeps softening. Plain fried boondi keeps two weeks airtight.
