Punjabi

Egg Bhurji

eggs scrambled hard with onion, chilli and tomato, the ten-minute dinner

gluten freenut freedairy freehealthier
Prep5 min
Cook12 min
Total17 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 5 Eggs
  • 1, finely chopped Onion
  • 1, finely chopped Tomato
  • 2, finely chopped Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp, grated Gingeroptional
  • 1/4 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp Chilli Powderoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • a handful Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 2 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Bhurji is deliberately dry and crumbly, not soft and creamy. Cook the eggs harder than you would for a Western scramble.
  • Salt the eggs only as they go in. Salted early, they weep.

Method

  1. Crackle the cumin in hot oil, then fry the onion 4 minutes until soft and just colouring.
  2. Add the green chilli and ginger, then the tomato, and cook 3 minutes until it breaks down.
  3. Add the turmeric and chilli powder and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Break the eggs straight into the pan, season, and stir constantly over medium heat.Straight into the pan, not beaten first: it gives the loose curds bhurji wants.
  5. Keep going 3 minutes until the eggs are dry and crumbly with no wet patches.
  6. Scatter coriander and eat immediately with pav or roti.

Storage

Eat straight away. Bhurji reheats badly and turns watery in the fridge.
