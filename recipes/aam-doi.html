Bengali

Aam Doi

mango folded into set yogurt, the summer version of mishti doi

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep15 min
Cook20 min
Active35 min
Plus4 hr chilling
Serves6
Difficultyeasy

Start the day before. The chilling alone is 4 hr, against 35 min of actual work.

Contains: milk

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 500g, hung 2 hours Yogurt
  • 400g, ripe, pureed Mangoripe and sweet, despite the ingredient name
  • 150g Condensed Milk
  • 2 tbsp Sugaroptional
  • 3 pods, seeds ground Cardamom

Cookware

steamer

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Use a sweet, fibreless mango and pass the puree through a sieve. Fibres ruin the texture.
  • The mango carries a lot of water, so the yogurt has to be hung longer than for plain bhapa doi.

Method

  1. Hang the yogurt 2 hours until quite firm.
  2. Puree the mango and pass it through a sieve.
  3. Whisk the yogurt with the condensed milk, cardamom and mango puree until even.
  4. Pour into a dish, cover with foil and steam 20 minutes until just set at the edges.
  5. Cool and refrigerate 4 hours before serving.It sets in the fridge, not in the steamer. Do not overcook it looking for firmness.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Fresh mango shortens its life compared with plain mishti doi, so eat it sooner.
