Awadhi/Lucknowi

Rabri

milk reduced for an hour with the skin folded back in at every stage

egg freegluten freevegetarian
Prep5 min
Cook75 min
Active80 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, tree nuts

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 2 litres, full fat Milkfull fat, and nothing less; skimmed milk will not layer
  • 80g Sugar
  • 5 pods, seeds ground Cardamom
  • a pinch Saffronoptional
  • 2 tbsp, slivered Pistachiosoptional
  • 2 tbsp, slivered Almondsoptional

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Rabri is not thickened milk, it is collected milk skin. The layers are the dish, so it should never be smooth.
  • Use the widest pan you own. More surface area means more skin, which means better rabri.

Method

  1. Bring the milk to a boil in a wide heavy pan, then lower to a bare simmer.
  2. As a skin forms, push it to the side of the pan with a spatula and leave it stuck there.Do not stir it back in. You are collecting layers on the wall of the pan.
  3. Keep going for about an hour, until the milk has reduced by three quarters.
  4. Add the sugar, cardamom and saffron and stir to dissolve.
  5. Scrape all the collected skin off the sides back into the milk and fold once, no more.Once. Stirring it smooth undoes the entire hour.
  6. Cool and refrigerate. Serve cold, topped with the nuts.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated. It is reduced dairy, so keep it cold and do not leave it out.
