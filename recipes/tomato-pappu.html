Andhra

Tomato Pappu

andhra everyday toor dal cooked soft with sour tomato and garlic

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freenut freehealthier
Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1 cup, rinsed Toor Dal
  • 3 medium, roughly chopped Tomatosour tomatoes make the dish
  • 1 medium, chopped Onionoptional
  • 4, slit Green ChilliAndhra pappu is meant to be hot
  • 6 cloves, crushed Garlic
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • pea-sized piece Tamarindonly if the tomatoes are not sour enoughoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 2, broken Dried Red Chilli
  • 1 sprig Curry Leaves
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 3 cups Water
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

pressure cooker, stovetop

Before you start

  • Rinse the toor dal until the water runs clear, which stops the dal frothing over.
  • Chop the tomatoes coarsely; they go into the cooker with the dal and melt away.
  • Crush the garlic with the flat of a knife rather than mincing it. You want soft sweet cloves at the end.

Method

  1. Pressure cook the dal with the tomatoes, green chilli, turmeric and 2.5 cups water for 5 whistles.Cooking the tomato with the dal, not after, is what makes it pappu rather than dal with tomato.
  2. Let the pressure drop naturally, open, and mash the dal against the side of the pot until smooth.
  3. Add the salt, chilli powder and tamarind if using, with 1/2 cup water, and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Adjust with hot water until it pours thickly off the ladle.
  5. Heat the oil in a small pan and pop the mustard seeds, then add the cumin.
  6. Add the crushed garlic and fry until the cloves turn golden at the edges, about 1 minute.Garlic browned properly here is most of the flavour; pale garlic tastes raw in the dal.
  7. Add the onion and fry 3 minutes until soft and lightly browned.
  8. Add the red chilli, curry leaves and hing and let them crackle for 15 seconds.
  9. Pour the tempering into the dal, stir once, scatter the coriander and serve over hot rice.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and thickens as it stands. Reheat with a splash of water and a fresh squeeze of lemon if it tastes tired.
