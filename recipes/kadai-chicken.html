Punjabi

Kadai Chicken

chicken with peppers in a coarse pounded coriander and chilli masala

dairy freeegg freegluten freehealthiernut free
Prep20 min
Cook35 min
Total55 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 800g bone-in pieces Chicken
  • 2, in 3cm squares Capsicum
  • 2, in petals Onion
  • 400g, chopped Tomato
  • 2 tbsp, coarsely pounded Coriander Powder
  • 5, pounded with the coriander Dried Red Chilli
  • 2 tbsp, half paste half julienne Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Kasuri Methi
  • 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
  • a handful Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 4 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Kadai masala is pounded coarse from whole coriander and dried chilli. Ready-ground powder gives a different, flatter dish.
  • Semi-dry is correct. If it is swimming, cook it down.

Method

  1. Dry-roast the coriander seed and dried chillies until fragrant, cool, and pound coarse.
  2. Sear the capsicum and onion petals on high for 2 minutes so they blister but stay firm. Lift out.
  3. Brown the chicken in the same pan, then add the ginger paste and garlic.
  4. Add the tomato and two-thirds of the pounded masala, cover, and cook 20 minutes until the chicken is done and the oil separates.
  5. Return the peppers and onion, add the rest of the masala, and toss on high for 3 minutes.High heat at the end drives off the last of the liquid.
  6. Finish with kasuri methi, garam masala, ginger julienne and coriander.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. The peppers soften overnight, which is a loss but not a disaster. Reheat hard and fast.
