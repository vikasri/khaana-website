Bengali

Macher Jhol

a thin turmeric and ginger fish broth built around fried rohu steaks

pescatariangluten freedairy freenut free
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: fish

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 600g rohu or katla, cut into 2cm cross-section steaks Fishany firm freshwater fish on the bone
  • 2 medium, quartered lengthwise Potato
  • 1, quartered Tomato
  • 1 small, in thick wedges Eggplantoptional
  • 2 tsp, divided Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1.5 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tbsp, grated to a paste Ginger
  • 1/2 tsp Nigella
  • 3, slit Green Chilli
  • 5 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt
  • 700ml Water

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Rub the fish steaks with 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp salt and leave 15 minutes at room temperature.
  • Pat the steaks completely dry before they go in the oil, or they will stick and tear.
  • Mix the remaining turmeric, chilli powder, cumin powder and ginger with 4 tbsp water into a loose paste so it does not scorch in the pan.

Method

  1. Heat the mustard oil in a kadhai until it smokes, then lower to medium-high.
  2. Slide in the fish steaks and fry 3 minutes a side until the surface is firm and browned.Do not move the fish for the first 3 minutes. It releases from the pan when it is ready.
  3. Lift the fish out onto a plate and leave the oil in the pan.
  4. Fry the potato wedges in the same oil for 5 minutes until golden at the edges, then add the eggplant for 2 minutes.
  5. Push the vegetables aside, add the nigella seeds to the clear space and let them pop for 10 seconds.
  6. Stir in the spice paste and fry 2-3 minutes until it darkens and stops smelling raw.If it sticks, add a tablespoon of water rather than more oil.
  7. Add the tomato, slit chillies, 700ml water and salt. Bring to a boil and simmer 12 minutes until the potato is tender.
  8. Return the fish steaks, spooning gravy over them, and simmer 4 minutes only.Overcooked fish goes cottony. Four minutes is enough. It finished cooking in the frying stage.
  9. Turn off the heat, scatter the coriander, and rest 5 minutes before serving with plain rice.

Storage

Keeps 1 day refrigerated. Reheat gently just to a simmer; the fish toughens if it boils again.
