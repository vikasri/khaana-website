Kheema Par Eeda

spiced mutton mince with eggs baked into the surface, soft yolks over dry kheema

gluten freehealthiernut free
Prep15 min
Cook40 min
Total55 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk, egg

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g, minced Mutton
  • 4 large Eggs
  • 2 large, finely chopped Onion
  • 2 medium, finely chopped Tomato
  • 1 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 3, finely chopped Green Chilli
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 3/4 tsp Garam Masala
  • a handful, chopped Coriander Leaves
  • 10 leaves, chopped Mintoptional
  • 3 tbsp Ghee
  • 1/2 tsp, coarsely ground Black Pepperfor cracking over the eggs
  • to taste Salt
  • 150ml Water

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Use a wide pan with a lid. You need enough surface area to sit four eggs apart from each other.
  • Bring the eggs to room temperature so the whites set evenly on the hot kheema.
  • The kheema must be dry before the eggs go on. Wet mince makes the whites run together into a puddle.

Method

  1. Heat the ghee in a wide kadhai and fry the onion until golden brown, 12 minutes.
  2. Add ginger, garlic and green chilli and fry 2 minutes.
  3. Add turmeric, chilli powder, coriander and cumin powder, and fry 30 seconds.
  4. Add the mince and break it up with the back of a spoon, frying 8 minutes until it loses all pinkness and starts to catch.Break the lumps early. Once the mince sets in clumps you will not get it smooth again.
  5. Add the tomato and salt and cook 6 minutes until the tomato disappears into the mince.
  6. Add the water, cover, and simmer 12 minutes, then uncover and cook off every trace of liquid, about 5 minutes.Dry means dry. Tilt the pan and no gravy should run.
  7. Stir through the garam masala, coriander leaves and mint, then level the surface and press four shallow wells with the back of a spoon.
  8. Crack an egg into each well, season with salt and the coarse black pepper.
  9. Cover and cook on the lowest heat 8-10 minutes, until the whites are opaque and the yolks still wobble.Check at 8 minutes. A minute too long and you lose the runny yolk that makes the dish.
  10. Bring the pan to the table and serve with pav or rotli.

Storage

Best eaten straight away for the soft yolks. Leftover kheema alone keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat and crack fresh eggs onto it.
