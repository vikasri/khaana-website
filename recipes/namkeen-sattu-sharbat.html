Namkeen Sattu Sharbat

savoury roasted gram drink with black salt, lemon and roasted cumin, drunk cold through the summer

dairy freeegg freegluten freehealthiernut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook10 min
Active20 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 6 tbsp, dry-roasted Satturoasted gram flour, the whole point of the drink
  • 500ml, well chilled Water
  • 1/2 tsp Black Saltthe sulphurous note that makes this taste right
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 Lemon
  • 1, very finely chopped Green Chillioptional
  • 2 tbsp, finely chopped Onionoptional
  • 8 leaves, torn Mintoptional
  • 1 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 1/4 tsp Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Sattu is sold already roasted, so it goes straight in. If you are standing in besan, dry-roast it over low heat for 8-10 minutes until it darkens a shade and smells like roasted grain, then cool it completely (unroasted besan tastes raw and pasty. It must be cold either way) hot flour will not disperse in cold water.
  • Chill the water beforehand. This is drunk cold, and a lukewarm sattu sharbat is heavy and dull.
  • Roast and crush the cumin fresh; pre-ground cumin powder has none of the smokiness this needs.

Method

  1. Dry-roast the cumin seeds in the same pan for about 1 minute, until they darken and pop, then crush them coarsely.
  2. Whisk the sattu with just 100ml of the chilled water to a completely smooth, thick paste.Paste first, then thin. Pour all the water in at once and you get lumps that never break up.
  3. Whisk in the remaining 400ml chilled water in a steady stream until the drink is thin and even.
  4. Stir in the black salt, plain salt, roasted crushed cumin and the juice of the whole lemon.
  5. Add the green chilli, onion, mint and coriander leaves and stir once.
  6. Taste and adjust. It should be sour and salty first, with the roasted flour underneath. Serve immediately in tall glasses.Sattu settles fast; give the glass a stir between sips or serve straight from the jug.

Storage

Drink within 20 minutes of mixing, before the flour settles and thickens. The roasted flour itself keeps a month in an airtight jar at room temperature, so only the mixing is last-minute.
