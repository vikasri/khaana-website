Kerala

Kozhi Porichathu

crisp fried chicken in a pepper-and-fennel masala with fried curry leaves

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut free
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Active45 min
Plus2 hr chilling
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 800g, bone-in pieces, skin removed and slashed twice each Chickenthighs and drumsticks stay juicier than breast
  • 3cm piece Ginger
  • 8 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 2 tsp, coarsely ground Black Peppercoarse, not fine. You want it to crunch
  • 1.5 tsp Fennel Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
  • 2 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 3 tbsp Rice Flourthe crust; do not substitute wheat flour
  • 3 sprigs Curry Leaves
  • 3, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 4, thinly sliced Shallotfried alongside as garnishoptional
  • 300ml for shallow frying Coconut Oil
  • 1.5 tsp, or to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Slash each chicken piece to the bone twice so the masala reaches the middle.
  • Marinate for at least 2 hours, ideally 6, in the fridge, 30 minutes gives you seasoned skin and bland meat.
  • Take the chicken out of the fridge 20 minutes before frying so it cooks through without burning outside.
  • Pat the marinated pieces to shed loose liquid just before they go into the oil.

Method

  1. Crush the ginger and garlic to a rough paste with a little salt.
  2. Mix the paste with the kashmiri chilli, chilli powder, turmeric, black pepper, fennel powder, garam masala, lemon juice and salt into a thick marinade.
  3. Rub it into the slashed chicken, working it into the cuts, cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours.Get the marinade inside the slashes with your fingers. Coating the outside only is why fried chicken tastes hollow.
  4. Just before frying, sprinkle the rice flour over the chicken and toss so each piece wears a thin, even dusting.Add the rice flour at the last moment. Left to sit it goes gluey and slides off in the oil.
  5. Heat the coconut oil in a kadhai to about 170C. A pinch of rice flour should sizzle steadily, not violently.
  6. Slide in the chicken in a single uncrowded layer and fry over medium heat 7-8 minutes on the first side without moving it.Leave it alone. Turning early tears the crust off the meat.
  7. Turn and fry 6-7 minutes on the second side until deep red-brown and the juices at the thickest part run clear.
  8. Lift onto a rack, not paper, and let the pieces drain while you fry the rest.
  9. Drop the curry leaves, slit green chillies and sliced shallots into the hot oil for 20-30 seconds until the leaves stop spluttering and turn glassy.Stand back and cover with a lid for the first few seconds. Curry leaves in hot oil spit hard.
  10. Scatter the fried aromatics over the chicken, squeeze over a little more lemon and serve hot.

Safe temperatures: Chicken and duck are done at 74°C / 165°F, taken at the thickest part and clear of the bone. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Best eaten within the hour. Keeps 2 days refrigerated; re-crisp in a 200C oven for 8 minutes rather than the microwave.
