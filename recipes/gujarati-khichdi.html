Gujarati Khichdi

soft rice and moong dal cooked to a porridge, eaten with kadhi and ghee

egg freegluten freehealthierno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Active35 min
Plus20 min soaking
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 200g, short grain Rice
  • 100g, split and yellow Moong Dal
  • 1.2 litres Waterfour parts liquid to one of grain, by volume
  • 3 tbsp Gheehalf in the pot, half poured over at the table
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 1 tsp, crushed Gingeroptional
  • 1, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 1/2 tsp, coarsely cracked Black Pepperoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, pressure cooker

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Rinse the rice and dal together in three changes of water until it runs clear, then soak 20 minutes and drain.
  • This khichdi is deliberately soft and loose. The grains should surrender, not stay separate. Do not cut the water.
  • Have the kadhi made and hot before the khichdi is done; khichdi stiffens fast as it sits.

Method

  1. Heat half the ghee in the pressure cooker and crackle the cumin seeds until they turn a shade darker.
  2. Add the asafoetida, ginger and green chilli and let them sizzle 10 seconds.
  3. Tip in the drained rice and dal and stir 2 minutes until every grain is glossed with ghee.This brief fry keeps the khichdi from turning to glue.
  4. Add the water, turmeric and salt and stir well, scraping the base.
  5. Close the cooker and cook for 4 whistles on medium heat, then let the pressure fall on its own.
  6. Open, whisk briefly and thin with hot water if it has set firm. It should slump off a spoon.Only hot water. Cold water added to hot khichdi makes it gluey.
  7. Crack the black pepper over, pour the remaining ghee on top and serve with kadhi.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. It sets solid when cold; reheat in a pan with a generous splash of hot water, breaking it up with a spoon.
