Awadhi/Lucknowi

Awadhi Paneer Korma

paneer in an ivory cashew and yogurt gravy, mild and heavily perfumed

egg freegluten freehealthiervegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Total50 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, tree nuts

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 400g, in 2cm cubes Paneer
  • 200g, whisked smooth Yogurt
  • 2 medium, thinly sliced Onion
  • 18, soaked 20 minutes in hot water Cashew
  • 1 tbsp, soaked Melon Seedsoptional
  • 4 tbsp Ghee
  • 3 tbsp Creamoptional
  • 2 tsp paste Ginger
  • 2 tsp paste Garlic
  • 2, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 1.5 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Fennel Powder
  • 4 green, powdered Cardamom
  • 1/4 tsp Mace
  • 1/2 tsp White Pepper
  • a pinch in 2 tbsp warm milk Saffronoptional
  • 1/4 tsp Kewra Watera few drops only; it takes over quicklyoptional
  • 250ml, warm Water
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, blender

Before you start

  • Soak the paneer cubes in warm salted water for 15 minutes while you build the gravy. They stay soft instead of turning rubbery.
  • Bring the yogurt to room temperature and whisk it completely smooth before it goes anywhere near the pan.
  • Grind the cashew paste first and keep it beside the stove. It goes in at a moment when you cannot leave the pan.

Method

  1. Fry the sliced onion in 2 tbsp ghee over medium heat until pale gold and soft, 8 minutes. Do not let it brown.Brown onion makes a fine korma, just not a white one. Pale gold is the target.
  2. Cool the onion and grind it with the soaked cashews, melon seeds and 4 tbsp water to a completely smooth paste.
  3. Heat the remaining 2 tbsp ghee, add the ginger and garlic pastes and fry 90 seconds until the raw edge is gone.
  4. Add the onion-cashew paste and cook on low for 6 minutes, stirring often, until it thickens and no longer smells raw.It catches easily. Keep the spoon moving and the flame low.
  5. Take the pan off the heat and whisk in the yogurt in three additions, then return to the lowest flame for 5 minutes until the ghee shows at the edges.
  6. Stir in the coriander powder, fennel powder, mace, white pepper, green chillies and salt and cook 2 minutes.
  7. Add 250ml warm water and simmer uncovered 6 minutes until the gravy coats a spoon.
  8. Drain the paneer, slip it in and simmer 4 minutes only.Longer than this and the paneer tightens up. It only needs to warm through.
  9. Finish with cream, cardamom powder, saffron milk and a few drops of kewra water. Rest covered 5 minutes before serving.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat on low with a splash of milk; the paneer firms up in the fridge and softens again as it warms. Do not freeze.
