Tamil Nadu

Medu Vada

crisp-shelled urad dal doughnuts, cloud-soft inside, peppery with ginger

dairy freeegg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep25 min
Cook25 min
Active50 min
Plus4 hr soaking
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Start the day before. The soaking alone is 4 hr, against 50 min of actual work.

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1 cup, skinless, soaked 4 hours Urad Dal
  • 1 tsp, coarsely crushed Black Pepper
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 tbsp, finely minced Ginger
  • 2, finely minced Green Chilli
  • 1 sprig, torn small Curry Leaves
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 1 tbsp Rice Flourinsurance for a crisper shell if the batter feels softoptional
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 500ml, for deep frying Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

kadhai, blender, stovetop

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Soak the urad dal 4 hours, no longer. Over-soaked dal drinks oil.
  • Drain it thoroughly and let it sit in the colander 10 minutes; wet dal needs extra water to grind and gives flat vadas.
  • Chill the ground batter 20 minutes if your kitchen is warm. Cold batter holds its shape on the fingers.
  • Add the salt only just before frying; salted batter goes watery within minutes.

Method

  1. Grind the drained dal with 2 to 3 tbsp of ice-cold water, in short bursts, to a thick fluffy paste.Every extra spoon of water costs you crispness. Scrape down rather than adding more.
  2. Beat the batter with your hand for 3 minutes until it lightens in colour and feels aerated.
  3. Test it by dropping a pinch into a bowl of water. It should float within seconds.If it sinks, beat for another 2 minutes; unaerated batter fries dense.
  4. Fold in the pepper, cumin, ginger, chilli, curry leaves, coriander, hing and salt.
  5. Heat the oil to 170C. A drop of batter should rise steadily, not shoot to the surface.
  6. Wet your palm, take a lime-sized ball of batter, flatten it and make a hole through the centre with a wet thumb.
  7. Slide the vada off your palm into the oil from close to the surface, and fry 4 or 5 at a time.
  8. Fry 4 to 5 minutes, turning once, until deep gold and the sizzle drops to a quiet crackle.That quietening means the water has cooked out and the shell has set.
  9. Drain on a rack rather than paper so the underside stays crisp, and serve immediately with coconut chutney.

Storage

Best within the hour. Leftovers keep a day refrigerated; refresh in a 180C oven for 6 minutes rather than a microwave, which turns them leathery.
