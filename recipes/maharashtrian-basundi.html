Maharashtrian Basundi

milk cooked down by half until it is thick, grainy and the colour of ivory

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook70 min
Active80 min
Plus3 hr chilling
Serves4

Per serving350 kcal

Allergens in Maharashtrian Basundi: milk, tree nuts.

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1.5 litres, full-fat Milkthe whole dish is the milk, so use the best you can get
  • 90g (about 7 tbsp) Sugar
  • 5 pods, seeds crushed Cardamom
  • a generous pinch, soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk Saffronoptional
  • a scraping Nutmegoptional
  • 10, blanched and slivered Almondsoptional
  • 10, slivered Pistachiosoptional

Cookware

stovetop

Ingredient names in English →

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Use the widest, heaviest pan you own. A wide surface is what reduces milk in an hour rather than two.
  • Rinse the pan with cold water and leave a film of it before pouring in the milk. It makes the base far less likely to catch.
  • Soak the saffron in warm milk for 15 minutes so it gives up its colour before it goes in.

Method

  1. Bring the milk to a boil in a wide heavy pan, then drop the heat to a steady low simmer.
  2. Simmer 45-55 minutes, stirring every 3-4 minutes and scraping the base and the sides with a flat spatula.Scrape the cream that sticks to the sides back into the milk each time. That is what makes basundi grainy rather than merely thick.
  3. Keep going until the milk has reduced by half and coats the back of a spoon in a thick ivory layer.Judge by volume, not the clock. Mark the starting level on your spatula handle if it helps.
  4. Stir in the sugar and simmer 8 more minutes. The milk will loosen at first and then thicken again.Adding sugar early slows the reduction and risks scorching. Wait until the milk is already thick.
  5. Add the crushed cardamom, the saffron milk and the scraping of nutmeg and simmer 2 minutes.
  6. Take off the heat and cool to room temperature, stirring occasionally to stop a skin forming.
  7. Chill at least 3 hours; it thickens noticeably as it cools.
  8. Serve cold in small bowls, scattered with the slivered almonds and pistachios, with hot puris alongside if it is a festival day.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated in a covered container. Serve cold; if it has set too stiff, loosen with 2 tbsp cold milk and stir well.
