Ragda Pattice

potato patties drowned in a thin white pea curry with chutneys on top

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep30 min
Cook45 min
Active75 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 250g, white, soaked overnight Dried Peas
  • 700g, boiled and grated Potato
  • 3 tbsp Cornflour
  • 1, finely chopped Onion
  • 4 tbsp sweet extract Tamarind
  • a handful Mint
  • a large handful Coriander Leaves
  • 3 Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp, grated Ginger
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 2 tsp Chaat Masala
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 6 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, pressure cooker, tawa

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • The ragda should be thin enough to pour, not a thick curry. It is a sauce for the pattice, not a dal.
  • Crush a ladle of the cooked peas against the pan to thicken it rather than adding flour.

Method

  1. Pressure cook the soaked peas with turmeric and salt for 5 whistles until completely soft.
  2. Temper cumin in oil, add ginger and chilli, then tip in the peas with their liquid.
  3. Crush a ladleful against the side of the pan and simmer 10 minutes to a pouring consistency.Pouring, not spooning. Add hot water if it tightens.
  4. Mix the grated potato with cornflour, chaat masala and salt, and shape into 8 patties.
  5. Shallow-fry the pattice on a tawa, 5 minutes a side, until a hard crust forms.
  6. Put two pattice in each bowl and ladle the hot ragda over them.
  7. Top with chopped onion, both chutneys, chaat masala and coriander.

Storage

The ragda keeps 3 days refrigerated and thickens; loosen with hot water. Pattice are best fresh but the raw shaped patties keep 2 days.
