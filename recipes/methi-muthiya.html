Methi Muthiya

steamed fenugreek dumplings squeezed by hand, then tossed in a sesame tempering

dairy freeegg freenut freeveganvegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Total50 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: gluten, sesame, mustard

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 150g, leaves picked and chopped Methi Leaves
  • 120g Atta
  • 60g Besan
  • 40g Jowar Flourfor a coarser, more traditional crumboptional
  • 2 tbsp Sesame
  • 1/2 tsp Ajwain
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 tsp, paste Ginger
  • 2, minced Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp juice Lemon
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Sodakeeps them lightoptional
  • 5 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 10 Curry Leaves
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetida
  • 2 tbsp, grated Coconutoptional
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, steamer, kadhai

Before you start

  • Salt the chopped methi and rest it 10 minutes, then squeeze hard. The water it gives up is usually enough to bind the dough with no extra liquid.
  • Oil the steamer plate well and oil your palms before shaping, or the dough sticks to everything.
  • Muthiya must cool completely before slicing, so build in 20 minutes of waiting before the tempering stage.

Method

  1. Mix the flours, sesame, ajwain, ground spices, sugar, salt, ginger, chilli and lemon juice in a bowl.
  2. Add the squeezed methi and 2 tbsp of the oil and work it into a stiff dough, using the methi water only if needed.Keep it firm. Slack dough collapses in the steamer.
  3. Work in the baking soda, then divide the dough into four and roll each into a log about 4cm thick, squeezing it in your fist so it keeps the finger marks that give muthiya its name.
  4. Steam the logs 20 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean and the surface is dry.
  5. Cool completely, at least 20 minutes, then cut into 2cm slices.Slicing warm muthiya tears it into crumbs.
  6. Heat the remaining oil in a kadhai, pop the mustard seeds, and add the curry leaves and asafoetida.
  7. Add the slices and pan-fry 6-8 minutes, turning occasionally, until the cut faces are golden and crisp.Turn rarely, not constantly. Each face needs contact time to colour.
  8. Scatter the coconut and coriander over and serve hot with tea or masala chai.

Storage

Steamed logs keep 4 days refrigerated. Slice and fry to order; fried muthiya keeps 2 days and crisps up again in a hot dry pan.
