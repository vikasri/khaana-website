Anglo-Indian

Duck Buffath

duck stewed with vinegar and whole spice, a Christmas table dish

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut free
Prep25 min
Cook90 min
Total115 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1.2kg, jointed, excess fat trimmed Duck
  • 4, thickly sliced Onion
  • 5 tbsp Vinegarbuffath is a vinegar stew; this is not a place to economise
  • 10 cloves, sliced Garlic
  • 2 tbsp, julienned Ginger
  • 6 Dried Red Chilli
  • 2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 stick Cinnamon
  • 6 Cloves
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 3, quartered Potatooptional
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Buffath is close kin to Goan vindaloo and sorpotel: meat, vinegar, garlic and long keeping. It sat on Anglo-Indian Christmas tables because it improved for days.
  • Render the duck fat off first and cook the onions in it. Nothing else gives the same depth.

Method

  1. Brown the duck pieces skin down in a dry heavy pot until the fat runs and the skin is deep gold. Lift out.No oil. There is more than enough fat in the bird.
  2. Pour off all but 4 tbsp of the fat. Fry the onions in it until soft and brown, 15 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, chillies, cinnamon, cloves, pepper and turmeric. Fry 3 minutes.
  4. Return the duck, add the vinegar and 400ml water, cover and simmer 70 minutes until the meat pulls easily.
  5. Add the potato for the last 25 minutes. Season with salt and the sugar.The sugar is not for sweetness, it takes the raw edge off the vinegar.
  6. Rest overnight if you can. It is a better dish on day two.

Storage

Keeps 4 days refrigerated and genuinely improves for the first two, which is the vinegar doing its work. Reheat to a full simmer. Freezes for a month.
