Pahari

Jhangora ki Kheer

barnyard millet simmered slowly in milk with jaggery and cardamom, grainy and gentle

vegetariangluten freeno onion garlic
Prep10 min
Cook50 min
Total60 min
Serves6
Difficultyeasy

Contains: dairy, nuts

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 1/2 cup (100g) Ricestand-in for jhangora, the barnyard millet the catalogue does not carry; use samak millet if you can find it
  • 1.5 litres, full fat Milk
  • 150g, grated Jaggeryor 120g sugar
  • 6 pods, seeds crushed Cardamom
  • 1 tsp Gheefor toasting the grain
  • 12, slivered Almondsoptional
  • 2 tbsp Raisinsoptional
  • a pinch, soaked in 1 tbsp warm milk Saffronoptional

Equipment

stovetop

Before you start

  • Rinse the grain in three changes of water until it runs clear, then soak 20 minutes and drain — this is soaking time, not cooking time.
  • Grate the jaggery and keep it ready; it must go in off the heat at the very end.
  • Use a heavy-based pan. Kheer sticks and scorches in a thin one, and burnt milk cannot be rescued.

Method

  1. Toast the drained grain in the ghee over medium heat for 2 minutes until each grain is glossy and smells faintly nutty.
  2. Pour in the milk, bring to a boil, then drop the heat to its lowest setting.Watch it as it comes up — milk climbs the pan and boils over in seconds.
  3. Simmer uncovered for 35-40 minutes, stirring every 4-5 minutes and scraping the bottom and sides of the pan with a flat spoon.Scrape the base every single time. A skin of milk left sitting there will catch and taint the whole pot.
  4. Keep going until the grains are soft and swollen and the milk has reduced by about a third, coating the spoon thickly.
  5. Stir in the almonds and raisins and cook 3 minutes more, until the raisins plump.
  6. Take the pan off the heat and let it stand for 8-10 minutes, until you can comfortably hold a hand against the side of the pan.This wait is not optional — jaggery added to near-boiling milk will curdle it instantly.
  7. Stir in the grated jaggery until fully dissolved, then the crushed cardamom and the saffron milk.
  8. Taste and add a little more jaggery if you want it sweeter, then rest 10 minutes for the flavours to settle.
  9. Serve warm, or chill 2 hours and serve cold — it thickens considerably in the fridge.

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated in a covered container. It sets firm when cold; loosen with 3-4 tbsp of warm milk and stir well before serving. Do not reheat over high heat.
