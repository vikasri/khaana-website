Bihari

Dhuska

jharkhandi deep-fried rice and chana dal discs, crisp outside and soft within

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Active50 min
Plus5 hr soaking
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Start the day before. The soaking alone is 5 hr, against 50 min of actual work.

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 1.5 cups, short-grain Rice
  • 0.5 cup Chana Dal
  • 1.5 tbsp, roughly chopped Ginger
  • 4 Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp Turmericgives dhuska its yellow
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • to taste Salt
  • 150 ml, for grinding Water
  • 700 ml, for deep-frying Neutral Oil
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional

Cookware

kadhai, stovetop, blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Soak the rice and chana dal together in plenty of water for at least 5 hours or overnight; they must crush easily between your nails before you grind.
  • Grind the batter at least 2 hours before frying and leave it at room temperature. It does not ferment, but resting makes the dhuska lighter.
  • Do not add extra water to the batter at the last minute; a slack batter drinks oil.

Method

  1. Drain the soaked rice and dal thoroughly, shaking the colander dry.
  2. Grind them with the ginger, green chillies and 150 ml water into a thick, slightly grainy batter. The texture of idli batter, not smooth.Add the water in stages. Too thin and the dhuska will not hold its shape in the oil.
  3. Beat in the turmeric, chilli powder, cumin seeds and salt, then rest the batter 2 hours covered.
  4. Stir in the coriander leaves and whisk the batter hard for 30 seconds to aerate it.
  5. Heat the oil in a kadhai to 180C. A drop of batter should rise and sizzle within 3 seconds.Too cool and they turn greasy; too hot and the centres stay raw.
  6. Slide in ladlefuls of batter, 3 at a time, easing each one into a rough 10cm disc as it hits the oil.
  7. Fry 2 minutes until they puff and float, then turn and fry another 2 to 3 minutes until deep gold and crisp at the edges.Wait for them to float before turning, or they collapse.
  8. Lift onto a rack, not paper, so the undersides stay crisp, and repeat with the rest of the batter.
  9. Serve hot with ghugni or aloo chokha.

Storage

Best eaten within the hour. Leftovers keep 1 day refrigerated; crisp them up in a hot oven or a dry pan for 4 minutes rather than the microwave.
