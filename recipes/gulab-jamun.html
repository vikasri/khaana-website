Awadhi/Lucknowi

Gulab Jamun

milk-solid dumplings fried slowly and soaked in cardamom syrup

vegetariannut free
Prep25 min
Cook40 min
Total65 min
Serves6
Difficultyadvanced

Contains: dairy, gluten

Ingredients

Quantities for 6.

  • 250g, grated Khoya
  • 50g Maida
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Soda
  • 3 tbsp, for binding Milk
  • 500g Sugar
  • 5, crushed Cardamom
  • 1 tsp Rose Wateroptional
  • a pinch Saffronoptional
  • 600ml Gheefor deep frying

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • The oil must be barely warm when the jamuns go in and rise slowly over ten minutes. Hot fat browns the outside while the centre stays raw dough.
  • The syrup should be of one-string consistency and warm, not boiling, when the jamuns go in.

Method

  1. Make the syrup: 500g sugar to 500ml water with the crushed cardamom, boiled 8 minutes to a slightly sticky, one-string syrup. Keep it warm.
  2. Knead the grated khoya with maida and baking soda, adding milk a teaspoon at a time, to a smooth soft dough. Do not overwork it.Overworked dough gives hard jamuns. Stop as soon as it is smooth.
  3. Roll into 18 smooth balls with no cracks, rolling between your palms with a little ghee.Any crack becomes a split in the oil.
  4. Heat the ghee to only 120C, barely warm, and slide the balls in.Barely warm. If they sizzle, the ghee is too hot.
  5. Let them rise on their own and colour slowly over 10 minutes, turning gently, until deep brown all over.
  6. Lift out, rest 2 minutes, then drop into the warm syrup.
  7. Soak at least 2 hours before serving. They will double in size.Soaking is not a finishing touch. It is half the cooking.

Storage

Keeps 4 days refrigerated in its syrup and is better on day two. Warm gently before serving. Freezes 2 months in syrup.
