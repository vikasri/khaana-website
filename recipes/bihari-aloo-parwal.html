Bihari

Aloo Parwal ki Tarkari

dry pointed gourd and potato sabzi tempered with panch phoron and mustard oil

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freenut freehealthier
Prep15 min
Cook25 min
Total40 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g, lightly scraped and halved lengthways Pointed Gourd
  • 3 medium, cut into thick fingers Potato
  • 4 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1.5 tsp Panch Phoron
  • 6 cloves, roughly crushed Garlic
  • 3, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1.5 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Amchuradded at the end
  • to taste Salt
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Scrape the parwal skin lightly with a knife rather than peeling it; the ridged skin holds the sabzi together.
  • Cut the potato fingers the same length as the parwal halves so they cook in step.
  • Keep the panch phoron measured out by the stove. It goes in and burns fast.

Method

  1. Heat the mustard oil in a kadhai until it smokes lightly, then drop the flame to medium.
  2. Add the panch phoron and wait 20 seconds for the fenugreek to darken and the fennel to smell sweet.Fenugreek in panch phoron turns bitter if it goes past brown. Move fast.
  3. Throw in the crushed garlic and green chillies and fry 40 seconds until the garlic is pale gold.
  4. Add the potato fingers and fry 5 minutes, turning, until the edges are translucent and starting to colour.
  5. Add the parwal and fry a further 5 minutes until the cut faces are blistered and browned in patches.Those browned faces are the flavour. Do not stir constantly or nothing colours.
  6. Sprinkle over the turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder and salt and toss for 1 minute.
  7. Add the 4 tbsp water, cover and cook on low for 10 minutes, shaking the pan twice.Cover but do not add more water. This is a dry sabzi and steam from the vegetables is enough.
  8. Uncover, check that a knife slides through the potato, and cook off any remaining moisture for 2 minutes.
  9. Stir through the amchur and coriander leaves, rest 2 minutes and serve with roti or dal-bhaat.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat uncovered in a hot pan for 3 minutes so it dries out again rather than steaming soft.
