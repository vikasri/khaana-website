Shankarpali

sweet fried diamonds of flour and semolina, made by the tinful at Diwali

egg freenut freevegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Total50 min
Serves25
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk, gluten

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 25.

  • 300g Maida
  • 50g, fine Ravathe semolina is what gives the bite
  • 150g Sugar
  • 150ml Milk
  • 80g, melted Ghee
  • 4 pods, seeds ground Cardamomoptional
  • 500ml for deep frying Neutral Oil
  • a pinch Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Fry on low. This is the one rule; shankarpali browned fast is raw in the middle and goes soft the next day.
  • The dough should be firm, closer to pastry than to chapati dough.

Method

  1. Warm the milk with the sugar until it dissolves, then cool. Stir in the melted ghee.
  2. Work in the flour, rava, cardamom and salt to a firm dough. Rest 20 minutes.
  3. Roll out 4mm thick and cut into diamonds.
  4. Fry on genuinely low heat, turning often, for about 8 minutes until pale gold throughout.Low and slow. If they colour in three minutes the oil is too hot.
  5. Drain and cool completely before storing.

Storage

Keeps 3 weeks in an airtight tin at room temperature.
