Chingri Malai Curry

prawns in a sweet cardamom-scented coconut milk gravy

pescatariangluten freenut freehealthier
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: fish, dairy

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g large, shelled and deveined, heads left on if you have them Prawns
  • 400ml, full fat Coconut Milk
  • 1 large, ground to a smooth paste Onion
  • 1 tbsp, grated to a paste Ginger
  • 1.5 tsp, divided Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
  • 4, bruised Cardamom
  • 2cm stick Cinnamon
  • 4 Cloves
  • 2 Bay Leaf
  • 2, slit Green Chillioptional
  • 3 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 1.5 tsp Sugarthe sweetness is characteristic, not a mistake
  • 1/4 tsp Garam Masalaoptional
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Rub the prawns with 1/2 tsp turmeric and 1/2 tsp salt and rest 10 minutes, no longer — salt draws water out of prawns fast.
  • Shake the tin of coconut milk hard, or whisk the thick and thin parts together, before measuring.
  • Grind the onion without water so the paste fries rather than stews.

Method

  1. Heat the mustard oil to smoking, cool for 30 seconds, then sear the prawns 45 seconds a side until they just turn pink at the edges. Lift them out immediately.They are not cooked through at this point and should not be. Prawns need 90 seconds total in the whole recipe.
  2. Add the bay leaves, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves to the same oil and let them puff for 20 seconds.
  3. Add the onion paste and fry 7-8 minutes on medium until it turns pale gold and the oil begins to separate.Do not brown it. This gravy should stay ivory-white, not go tan.
  4. Stir in the ginger paste, the remaining turmeric and the kashmiri chilli and cook 1 minute.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk with the sugar and salt, stirring as you go.
  6. Simmer on low, uncovered, for 6-7 minutes until the gravy coats the back of a spoon.Keep it below a rolling boil or the coconut milk will separate into grains and oil.
  7. Return the prawns with the slit green chillies and cook exactly 2 minutes.The moment a prawn curls into a tight O it is overdone. A loose C is right.
  8. Turn off the heat, stir in the ghee and garam masala, and cover for 5 minutes before serving with plain rice.

Storage

Keeps 1 day refrigerated. Reheat gently over low heat only until warm — reheating hard makes the prawns rubbery and splits the coconut milk.
