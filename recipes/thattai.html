Tamil Nadu

Thattai

flat, crisp rice discs studded with dal and curry leaf

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep25 min
Cook30 min
Active55 min
Serves20
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, sesame

Ingredients

Quantities for 20.

  • 300g Rice Flour
  • 3 tbsp, soaked 1 hour and drained Chana Dalthe studs; they should stay whole and crunchy
  • 2 tbsp, roasted and ground Urad Dal
  • 3 sprigs, torn Curry Leaves
  • 1 tbsp Sesame
  • 1/2 tsp Asafoetida
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 40g Butter
  • as needed Water
  • 500ml for deep frying Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Thattai means flat. They are pressed thin by hand between plastic sheets, not rolled.
  • Prick each disc before frying or it puffs into a ball.

Method

  1. Mix the flours, soaked chana dal, torn curry leaves, sesame, asafoetida, chilli powder, salt and butter.
  2. Add water gradually to a stiff dough. It should not be sticky.
  3. Take small balls and flatten each between two greased plastic sheets into thin discs.
  4. Prick each disc several times with a fork.This is not optional. Unpricked thattai balloons and cooks unevenly.
  5. Fry on medium until the bubbling subsides and they are pale gold and rigid, about 4 minutes.
  6. Drain and cool completely before storing.

Storage

Keeps 3 weeks in an airtight tin at room temperature. If they soften, 5 minutes in a low oven brings them back.
