Awadhi/Lucknowi

Paneer Do Pyaza

paneer with onion twice over, once melted into the gravy and once in soft petals

egg freegluten freehealthiernut freevegetarian
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Active45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 400g, in thick fingers Paneer
  • 5, two chopped fine and three in petals Onionthe name means two onions, and it is literal
  • 300g, chopped Tomato
  • 100g, whisked Yogurt
  • 1 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1.5 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tbsp Kasuri Methi
  • 4 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • a handful Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • The two onions do different jobs. The chopped one dissolves into the gravy; the petals go in at the end and must stay separate and sweet.
  • Take the yogurt off the heat before it goes in, or it splits.

Method

  1. Fry the chopped onion in oil for 12 minutes until deep brown, then add ginger and garlic.
  2. Add the ground spices with a splash of water, then the tomato, and cook until the oil separates.
  3. Take the pan off the heat, whisk in the yogurt, and only then return it to a low flame.Off the heat, always. Yogurt added to a hot pan curdles instantly.
  4. Simmer 5 minutes with a little water to a thick gravy.
  5. In a separate pan sear the onion petals hard for 2 minutes so they colour but keep their bite, and add the paneer for a minute.
  6. Fold the petals and paneer through the gravy, add kasuri methi and garam masala, and rest 5 minutes off the heat.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. The onion petals soften overnight, which loses the contrast the dish is named for.
