Batata Bhaji

soft turmeric-yellow potatoes with mustard seeds, curry leaves and green chilli

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freenut freehealthier
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Total25 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g, boiled, peeled and roughly broken by hand Potato
  • 1 medium, thinly sliced Onion
  • 3, slit Green Chilli
  • 3 tbsp Groundnut Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 12 Curry Leaves
  • 3/4 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 1/2 tsp Sugaroptional
  • 100ml Water
  • 1/2 Lemon
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Boil the potatoes the night before if you can — cold boiled potato breaks into rough chunks instead of turning to mash.
  • Break the potatoes by hand rather than cutting them; the ragged edges hold the tempering.

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes whole in their skins until a knife goes through easily, about 20 minutes, then cool, peel and break into rough 3cm pieces.Cook them whole and unpeeled or they take on water and go slimy.
  2. Heat the oil and pop the mustard seeds, then add the cumin, curry leaves and hing.
  3. Add the green chilli and sliced onion and cook 5 minutes until the onion is soft and translucent.
  4. Stir in the turmeric and let it cook 20 seconds in the oil.
  5. Add the potato, salt and sugar and turn everything over gently until the pieces are evenly yellow.Fold with a flat spatula from underneath. Stirring turns this into mashed potato.
  6. Pour in the water, cover and steam on low heat for 5 minutes.
  7. Squeeze over the lemon, fold through the coriander and serve with pooris, chapati or as the filling for vada pav.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat covered over low heat with 2 tbsp water; it also makes the best possible sandwich filling cold.
