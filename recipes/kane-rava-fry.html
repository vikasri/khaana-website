Kane Rava Fry

ladyfish rubbed in red chilli paste and shallow fried in a sandy semolina crust

dairy freeegg freenut freepescatarian
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Active40 min
Plus30 min marinating
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: gluten, fish

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 8 whole ladyfish, cleaned and slit down both sides Fishkane is the local name; small pomfret or mackerel fillets fry the same way
  • 8 dried, soaked 20 minutes in warm water Kashmiri Chilli
  • 5 cloves Garlic
  • 1/2 inch piece Ginger
  • marble-sized ball Tamarind
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1, juiced Lemon
  • 1 cup, fine Ravathis is wheat semolina, so the dish is not gluten-free
  • 3 tbsp Rice Flourmixed into the rava, it keeps the crust crisp as the fish coolsoptional
  • 6 tbsp, for shallow frying Coconut Oil
  • 1 sprig Curry Leavesfried in the last of the oil and scattered overoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, tawa

Before you start

  • Rinse the fish, pat it thoroughly dry inside and out, and score the thicker fish twice on each side.
  • Grind the masala first, then marinate the fish for 30 minutes in the fridge. Much longer and the lemon starts cooking the flesh.
  • Spread the rava on a wide plate so you can press the fish into it in one motion.

Method

  1. Grind the soaked chillies, garlic, ginger, tamarind, turmeric and pepper with 2 tbsp of the soaking water to a thick paste.
  2. Mix in the lemon juice and 1 tsp salt, then rub the paste into the fish, working it into the slits.
  3. Marinate 30 minutes in the fridge while you get the pan and the rava plate ready.
  4. Stir the rice flour into the rava on a wide plate.
  5. Heat 3 tbsp coconut oil in a flat tawa until a pinch of rava dropped in sizzles immediately.If the oil is not hot enough the crust drinks it and goes soggy instead of crisping.
  6. Press each fish into the rava on both sides, shake off the loose grains and lay it straight into the pan.Coat one fish at a time. Rava left sitting on a wet marinade turns to paste.
  7. Fry 4 minutes on the first side without touching it, until the crust is set and deep red-gold at the edges.Moving it early is what tears the crust off. Wait until it releases from the pan on its own.
  8. Flip once and fry 3 minutes on the second side, adding the remaining oil around the edge of the pan as needed.
  9. Fry the curry leaves in the last of the hot oil for 15 seconds and scatter them over the fish.
  10. Drain on a rack, not on paper, and serve hot with raw onion and lemon.A rack keeps the underside crisp; paper traps steam and softens it.

Safe temperatures: Fish is done at 63°C / 145°F, or when it turns opaque and flakes easily. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Eat within the hour. This is a dish that does not keep. Leftovers will sit 1 day refrigerated and are best crisped up in a dry pan, never in a microwave.
