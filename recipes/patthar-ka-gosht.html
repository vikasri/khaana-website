Hyderabadi

Patthar Ka Gosht

thin mutton slices seared hard and fast, smoky at the edges and still juicy

egg freegluten freehealthiernut free
Prep25 min
Cook20 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 600g leg, sliced into 5mm escalopes and flattened Muttonask the butcher for boneless leg cut thin
  • 2 tbsp paste Raw Papayathe tenderiser; skip only if using very young meat
  • 4 tbsp, thick Yogurt
  • 1 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 4 Green Chilli
  • 1/2 cup, packed Coriander Leaves
  • 1/4 cup leaves Mint
  • 1.5 tsp, coarsely ground Black Pepper
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powderoptional
  • 1, half in the marinade and half to serve Lemon
  • 3 tbsp, for the griddle Ghee
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, tawa, blender

Before you start

  • Marinate 2 hours refrigerated, and no longer than 3. Papaya keeps working, and overnight it turns the meat pasty.
  • Flatten each slice between two sheets of cling film with a rolling pin until about 4mm thick. Even thickness matters more than any spice here.
  • Bring the meat to room temperature 20 minutes before cooking, or the outside chars before the centre is done.

Method

  1. Grind the green chillies, coriander leaves and mint with 1 tbsp water to a coarse green paste.
  2. Mix the papaya paste, yogurt, ginger and garlic pastes, green paste, pepper, garam masala, cumin, chilli powder, juice of half a lemon and salt.
  3. Coat the flattened mutton slices in the marinade, cover, and refrigerate 2 hours.
  4. Heat a heavy tawa or cast-iron griddle over high heat for a full 5 minutes, until a drop of water skitters and vanishes.A properly hot griddle is the whole dish; the name comes from cooking it on a heated stone slab. A lukewarm pan stews the meat grey.
  5. Brush the griddle with ghee and lay on the slices in a single layer, shaking off excess marinade. Do not crowd the pan.Work in batches of three or four. Crowding drops the temperature and floods the pan with marinade.
  6. Sear 2 minutes without moving them, until the underside is browned and lifts cleanly.
  7. Flip, dot with a little more ghee, and cook 2 minutes more. The slices should feel springy, not firm.
  8. Move the cooked slices to a warm plate and repeat with the rest, wiping the griddle between batches.
  9. Rest 3 minutes, then squeeze over the remaining lemon and serve hot with sliced onion and mint chutney.

Storage

Best eaten within minutes of leaving the griddle. Cooked slices keep a day refrigerated and are decent in a roti roll; reheat 30 seconds a side on a hot tawa, never in a microwave.
