Sweet or Salted Lassi

yogurt churned with water until light and foaming, sweet or salted

egg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freevegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep5 min
Cook0 min
Active5 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Contains: milk

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 400g, cold and not too sour Yogurt
  • 200ml, cold Water
  • 3 tbsp Sugarfor the sweet versionoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Saltfor the salted versionoptional
  • 1 tsp, roasted Cumin Powdersalted version onlyoptional
  • 1/4 tsp Black Saltsalted version onlyoptional
  • 2, ground Cardamomsweet version onlyoptional
  • a few drops Rose Watersweet version onlyoptional

Cookware

blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Sweet and salted are not variations of one drink; they are two drinks. Do not try to make something in between.
  • The froth is the point. Churn hard, and serve before it settles.

Method

  1. Whisk the cold yogurt alone until completely smooth, with no lumps.
  2. Add the cold water gradually, whisking or blending, until it lightens and a head of foam forms.Gradually. Dumping the water in gives a thin, flat drink.
  3. For sweet lassi, add sugar, ground cardamom and a drop of rose water.
  4. For salted, add salt, black salt and roasted cumin instead.
  5. Churn once more to raise the foam and pour into chilled glasses.
  6. Serve at once, with the foam still standing.

Storage

Drink fresh. It separates within an hour and never quite recovers.
