Chicken Xacuti

dark roasted-coconut curry heavy with whole spice, the Goan Catholic Sunday chicken

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut free
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep25 min
Cook50 min
Active75 min
Plus30 min marinating
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 900g bone-in, in curry pieces Chicken
  • 1 cup grated fresh Coconutfrozen grated works; desiccated does not
  • 10 Kashmiri, stalks removed Dried Red Chilli
  • 2 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 tbsp Poppy Seedsthickens the gravy
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 6 Cloves
  • 3cm piece Cinnamon
  • 1 Star Anisethe spice that makes xacuti smell like xacuti
  • 1/4 tsp, grated Nutmeg
  • 1 blade Maceoptional
  • 1 tsp Stone Flowerdagad phool, for the earthy back noteoptional
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 3 medium, finely sliced Onion
  • 1 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 1 tbsp thick pulp Tamarind
  • 3 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • a small handful, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai, blender

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Marinate the chicken with the turmeric, half the ginger-garlic paste and salt for 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
  • Soak the tamarind in 3 tbsp hot water and squeeze out the pulp before you start; you will want it at the end.
  • Grate the coconut fresh if you possibly can. The whole dish is built on the flavour of coconut browned in a dry pan.

Method

  1. Dry-roast the coconut in a kadhai over low heat, stirring constantly, 10-12 minutes until it is the colour of milk chocolate. Tip it out onto a plate.Low heat and never stop stirring. Coconut goes from golden to burnt in under a minute, and burnt coconut cannot be rescued.
  2. In the same pan roast the chillies, coriander powder, cumin, poppy seeds, pepper, cloves, cinnamon, star anise, mace and stone flower for 2 minutes until fragrant.Add the coriander powder last. Ground spice scorches far faster than whole seed.
  3. Grind the roasted coconut and spices with the nutmeg and about 200ml warm water to a very smooth, thick paste. Give it a full 3-4 minutes in the blender.Any grittiness left now stays gritty in the finished curry.
  4. Heat the coconut oil and fry the sliced onion 12 minutes until deep gold and collapsing.
  5. Add the remaining ginger-garlic paste and fry 2 minutes until the raw smell lifts.
  6. Add the marinated chicken and turn it over high heat 6-8 minutes until the pieces are sealed and no longer pink outside.
  7. Stir in the ground masala and cook it down 8-10 minutes, stirring, until it darkens and oil beads at the edges.This is where xacuti gets its depth. If it catches, add a splash of water rather than lowering the flame.
  8. Pour in 400ml hot water, season with salt, cover and simmer 20 minutes until the chicken is tender and the gravy coats a spoon.
  9. Stir in the tamarind pulp, simmer 3 minutes more, and taste for salt and sourness. Finish with coriander leaves.

Safe temperatures: Chicken and duck are done at 74°C / 165°F, taken at the thickest part and clear of the bone. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and is better on day two once the roasted coconut settles. Reheat gently with a splash of hot water; freezes 2 months.
