Goan

Bebinca

Goa's layered coconut and egg cake, one grilled layer at a time

vegetariannut free
Prep25 min
Cook90 min
Total115 min
Serves8
Difficultyadvanced

Contains: dairy, gluten

Ingredients

Quantities for 8.

  • 400ml thick Coconut Milk
  • 8 yolks Eggs
  • 1.5 cups Maida
  • 1.5 cups Sugar
  • 200g, melted Ghee
  • 1/2 tsp Nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp Cardamomoptional
  • a pinch Salt

Equipment

oven

Before you start

  • Bebinca is a test of patience: each layer is baked separately, and a traditional one runs to sixteen. Seven still tastes right.
  • Ghee is brushed between layers. That fat is what lets them separate cleanly when sliced.

Method

  1. Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale, then beat in the coconut milk, flour, nutmeg, cardamom and salt to a smooth, pourable batter. Rest 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 180C with the grill element on. Brush a deep tin generously with ghee and warm it.
  3. Pour in a thin ladle of batter — about 5mm — and grill 8-10 minutes until the top is browned and set.Browned, not merely set. The colour contrast between layers is the whole look.
  4. Brush the baked layer with ghee, pour the next ladle over, and grill again. Repeat for at least seven layers.
  5. Cool completely in the tin, then turn out. Slicing warm will tear the layers apart.At least 3 hours. Overnight is better.
  6. Serve at room temperature in thin wedges.

Storage

Keeps a week in an airtight tin at room temperature and improves as it settles. Do not refrigerate — the ghee sets hard and the texture goes waxy.
