Hyderabadi

Hyderabadi Chicken 65

twice-fried chilli chicken finished in a spitting curry-leaf and yogurt tempering

gluten freenut free
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: dairy

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 600g boneless thigh, cut into 3cm cubes Chickenthigh stays juicy where breast dries out
  • 4 tbsp, thick, plus 3 tbsp for the finish Yogurt
  • 1 tbsp, paste Ginger
  • 1 tbsp, paste Garlic
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri Chillifor colour without punishing heat
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 3 tbsp Rice Flour
  • 2 tbsp Besan
  • 1, beaten Eggshelps the coating clingoptional
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp, coarsely ground Black Pepper
  • 1, juiced Lemon
  • 4 sprigs Curry Leavesgenerous. They are half the dish
  • 5, slit Green Chilli
  • 500ml for frying, plus 2 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Marinate the chicken for at least 2 hours, or overnight in the fridge. An hour is not enough for the yogurt to do its work.
  • Bring the marinated chicken back to room temperature for 20 minutes before frying, or the oil temperature will crash.
  • Pat the curry leaves completely dry; wet leaves turn hot oil into a volcano.

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the 4 tbsp yogurt, ginger and garlic pastes, kashmiri chilli, chilli powder, garam masala, black pepper, lemon juice and salt. Refrigerate at least 2 hours.
  2. Half an hour before cooking, work the rice flour, besan and beaten egg into the marinade. It should coat each piece in a thin paste, not a batter.Add the flours late. Mixed in early they draw water out and go sludgy.
  3. Heat the frying oil in a kadhai to 170C. A cube of chicken should sizzle steadily and rise within 3 seconds.
  4. Fry in three batches, 4-5 minutes each, until pale gold and just cooked through. Drain on a rack, not paper.Crowding the pan drops the oil temperature and gives soggy, greasy chicken.
  5. Raise the oil to 190C and return all the chicken for a second fry of 1-2 minutes until deep red-gold and crisp at the edges. Drain.
  6. Pour off the frying oil, leaving 2 tbsp in the pan. Drop in the curry leaves and slit green chillies and let them crackle for 20 seconds.
  7. Whisk the remaining 3 tbsp yogurt smooth, take the pan off the heat and stir it in.Off the heat, always. Yogurt added to a hot pan splits into curds and whey.
  8. Return the pan to a low flame, add the fried chicken and toss for 1 minute until every piece is coated and the yogurt has almost dried onto it.
  9. Finish with coriander leaves and serve hot with raw onion rings and lemon.

Storage

Best within the hour. It softens as it sits. Leftovers keep 2 days refrigerated; crisp them again in a hot dry pan or a 200C oven for 6 minutes rather than a microwave.
