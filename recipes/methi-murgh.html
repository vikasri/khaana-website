Hyderabadi

Methi Murgh

chicken braised with fresh fenugreek, bitter-sweet and thick rather than soupy

egg freegluten freenut free
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep20 min
Cook35 min
Active55 min
Plus20 min resting
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 800g bone-in curry cut Chickenthigh and drumstick hold up better than breast
  • 300g, picked from the stems Methi Leaves
  • 3 medium, finely sliced Onion
  • 150g, whisked smooth Yogurt
  • 1.5 tbsp paste Ginger
  • 1.5 tbsp paste Garlic
  • 4, slit lengthways Green Chilli
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1.5 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 3/4 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp, crushed Kasuri Methideepens the fenugreek note at the endoptional
  • 4 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 1 tbsp, to finish Gheeoptional
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • half, juiced Lemonoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Toss the picked methi leaves with 1 tsp salt, leave 20 minutes, then squeeze out the dark liquid hard. This is what stops the dish turning harshly bitter.
  • Take the yogurt out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead. Cold yogurt splits the moment it hits a hot pan.
  • Pat the chicken dry so it browns instead of steaming.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a kadhai and fry the sliced onion over medium heat until evenly golden brown, 12-14 minutes.Stir every couple of minutes and take it to gold, not mahogany. Burnt onion turns the gravy bitter and there is no rescuing it.
  2. Add the ginger and garlic pastes and fry 2 minutes, until the raw smell goes and the oil looks clear at the edges.
  3. Add the chicken and green chillies and sear over high heat 5 minutes, until the pieces are opaque all over.
  4. Lower the heat, stir in the turmeric, chilli powder, coriander and cumin powders with 2 tbsp water, and fry 3 minutes until the oil separates.The splash of water keeps the ground spices from scorching in the few seconds before the chicken juices come out.
  5. Take the pan off the heat for 30 seconds, whisk in the yogurt a spoonful at a time, then return to low heat and cook 5 minutes.Off the heat and a spoon at a time. This is the only reliable way to keep yogurt from curdling into grains.
  6. Fold in the squeezed methi leaves and salt, and cook uncovered 8 minutes, until the leaves collapse and darken.
  7. Cover and simmer on low 12 minutes, stirring twice, until the chicken is tender and the gravy clings to the pieces.
  8. Uncover and cook off any watery liquid over medium heat, 3-4 minutes. Methi murgh should be thick enough to scoop, not ladle.
  9. Sprinkle over the garam masala and crushed kasuri methi, spoon on the ghee, and rest covered 5 minutes off the heat.
  10. Finish with coriander leaves and lemon juice, and serve with rumali roti or plain rice.

Safe temperatures: Chicken and duck are done at 74°C / 165°F, taken at the thickest part and clear of the bone. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated and tastes better on day two. Reheat gently on the stovetop with 2 tbsp water; the microwave dries the chicken out.
