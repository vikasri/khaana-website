Crispy Chilli Baby Corn

battered baby corn tossed in a hot garlic sauce, the vegetarian chilli chicken

dairy freeegg freenut freevegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Total40 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: gluten, soy

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 300g baby corn, halved lengthways Corn
  • 5 tbsp Cornflour
  • 3 tbsp Maida
  • 2 tbsp, chopped Garlic
  • 4, slit Green Chilli
  • 1, cut into squares Capsicum
  • 1, cut into petals Onion
  • 4, chopped Spring Onion
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1 tbsp Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 500ml for deep frying, plus 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Baby corn holds water. Blanch it and dry it properly or the batter slides off.
  • The batter should coat like thin cream. Thick batter gives a doughy shell.

Method

  1. Blanch the baby corn 2 minutes in salted water, drain and dry thoroughly on a cloth.
  2. Whisk the cornflour, maida, salt and enough cold water into a thin batter. Coat the corn.
  3. Deep fry on medium-high until crisp and pale gold, 4 minutes. Drain.
  4. In 3 tbsp oil, fry the garlic and green chilli hard for 30 seconds, then the onion and capsicum for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the soy sauce, vinegar, chilli powder, pepper and a splash of water. Boil 1 minute.
  6. Toss in the corn, coat, and finish with spring onion.

Storage

Eat immediately. This does not keep in any state worth serving.
