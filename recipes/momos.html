Northeast Indian

Momos

Steamed dumplings with a juicy filling, eaten with a fiery tomato-chilli chutney

dairy freeegg freenut free
Prep45 min
Cook20 min
Active65 min
Plus30 min resting
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: gluten

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 2 cups Maida
  • 400g, minced Chickenor finely chopped cabbage for a vegetarian version
  • 1 cup, very finely chopped Cabbage
  • 1, very finely chopped Onion
  • 3, chopped Spring Onionoptional
  • 1 tbsp Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Ginger
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 6 Dried Red Chillifor the chutney
  • 3 Tomatofor the chutney
  • 2 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, steamer, blender

Before you start

  • Everything in the filling must be chopped very fine and kept raw. It cooks in the steamer, and any large piece will tear the wrapper.
  • Do not salt the filling until just before you fill: salt draws water out of the cabbage and makes the dough soggy.

Method

  1. Knead the flour with water and a pinch of salt into a firm, smooth dough. Rest 30 minutes covered.
  2. Mix the filling ingredients with the oil and pepper. Salt it only when you are ready to fill.
  3. Roll the dough very thin and cut 8cm rounds, keeping the edges thinner than the centre.Thin edges, thicker middle. Otherwise the pleated top is doughy.
  4. Place a spoon of filling in each, then pleat and seal into a half-moon or a gathered purse.
  5. Steam in a greased steamer for 10-12 minutes. The wrappers turn translucent and stop looking floury.Do not crowd them; they swell and stick together.
  6. For the chutney, boil the tomatoes and dried chillies, then blend with garlic and salt. Serve alongside.

Safe temperatures: Chicken and duck are done at 74°C / 165°F, taken at the thickest part and clear of the bone. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Best straight from the steamer. Uncooked momos freeze well on a tray for a month. Steam from frozen, adding 4 minutes. Cooked ones go rubbery.
