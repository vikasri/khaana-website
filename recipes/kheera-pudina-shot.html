Cucumber Mint Shot

the cooling one, a short glass of jal-jeera without the jeera

dairy freeegg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freeveganvegetarianwellness shot
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep10 min
Cook0 min
Active10 min
Serves2
Difficultyeasy

Allergens in Cucumber Mint Shot: none of the ten listed below.

Ingredients

Quantities for 2.

  • 1 large, peeled and chopped Cucumber
  • 20 leaves Mint
  • 1, juiced Lemon
  • ½ tsp Black Salt
  • ¼ tsp, roasted Cumin Powderoptional

Cookware

blender

Ingredient names in English →

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Method

  1. Blend the cucumber and mint with 50ml water.
  2. Strain through a sieve. Cucumber gives up its juice easily, so do not overwork it.
  3. Stir in the lemon juice, black salt and roasted cumin.
  4. Chill briefly and serve. It should taste of salt as much as of cucumber.

Storage

Same day. Cucumber juice separates and turns flat overnight.
