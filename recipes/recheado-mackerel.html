Goan

Recheado Mackerel

whole mackerel stuffed with red vinegar masala and shallow-fried crisp

dairy freeegg freenut freepescatarian
Prep25 min
Cook20 min
Active45 min
Plus30 min soaking
Serves4
Difficultymoderate

Contains: gluten, fish

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 4 whole mackerel, about 200g each, cleaned and slit along the belly Fishpomfret or lady fish work the same way
  • 12 dried, stemmed Kashmiri Chilli
  • 3 hot ones Dried Red Chillioptional
  • 100ml VinegarGoan coconut vinegar preferred
  • 10 cloves Garlic
  • 3cm piece Ginger
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 6 Cloves
  • 2cm stick Cinnamon
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 3/4 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tbsp thick pulp Tamarind
  • 2 tsp Sugar
  • 4 tbsp fine semolina, for coating Ravaswap for rice-flour if you need this gluten-free
  • 5 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt
  • 1, in wedges Lemonoptional

Cookware

stovetop, blender, tawa

Before you start

  • Make the recheado paste a day ahead if you can. It sharpens as it sits, and a jar in the fridge turns a weeknight into dinner in 20 minutes.
  • Soak the chillies in the vinegar 30 minutes before grinding.
  • Rub the cleaned fish with salt and turmeric and rest 15 minutes, then wipe dry. Dry skin is the whole game for a crisp fry.

Method

  1. Grind the soaked chillies, garlic, ginger, cumin, cloves, cinnamon, pepper, turmeric, tamarind, sugar and 1 tsp salt with the vinegar to a thick, smooth paste.It should be spreadable, like tomato puree. Not pourable. Add vinegar a spoon at a time, never water.
  2. Slit each mackerel along the belly and, with a small knife, open a second pocket along the backbone from the outside.
  3. Pack 1.5 tbsp of paste into each fish, inside the belly and into the pocket, and smear a thin film over the skin.
  4. Rest the stuffed fish 20 minutes on a plate in the fridge.This sets the paste so it stays in the fish rather than in the pan.
  5. Spread the rava on a plate and press each fish into it on both sides, shaking off the loose grains.
  6. Heat the oil on a flat tawa over medium heat until a grain of rava sizzles on contact.
  7. Lay in the fish and fry 5-6 minutes without moving it, until the underside is crusted and deep red-brown.Do not poke or shuffle. Let the crust form or it will stick and tear.
  8. Turn once with a wide spatula and fry another 4-5 minutes.
  9. Drain briefly on paper and serve hot with lemon wedges, rice and a plain dal.

Safe temperatures: Fish is done at 63°C / 145°F, or when it turns opaque and flakes easily. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Fried fish is best within the hour and does not reheat well. The recheado paste itself keeps 2 months refrigerated under a layer of oil in a dry jar.
