Tamatar Chokha

charred tomatoes crushed with garlic, chilli and mustard oil into a smoky relish

dairy freeegg freegluten freehealthiernut freeveganvegetarian
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Total25 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: mustard

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 6 medium, about 700g, ripe and firm Tomato
  • 6 cloves, peeled Garlic3 roasted, 3 crushed raw
  • 3, finely chopped Green Chilli
  • 1 small, finely chopped Onion
  • 2 tbsp, raw Mustard Oil
  • to taste Salt
  • 0.25 tsp Black Saltoptional
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leaves
  • 1 tsp, finely grated Gingeroptional

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Use ripe tomatoes with taut skin; watery ones turn the chokha into soup.
  • Thread 3 garlic cloves onto a skewer so you can char them on the flame alongside the tomatoes.
  • Have the onion and chilli chopped before you start roasting. This comes together in two minutes once the tomatoes are off the flame.

Method

  1. Set the tomatoes directly on a medium gas flame, resting them on the trivet.
  2. Roast 10 to 12 minutes, turning with tongs, until the skins are blackened and split and the tomatoes slump.Let them blacken properly. Pale roasting gives you sauce, not chokha.
  3. Char the skewered garlic for the last 3 minutes until the cloves are spotted brown and soft.
  4. Rest 5 minutes, then slip off and discard the blackened skins, saving any juice on the plate.
  5. Mash the tomato flesh and roasted garlic together with a fork into a coarse pulp.
  6. Crush the 3 raw garlic cloves with a pinch of salt and stir them in with the ginger.Raw garlic is what separates this from a cooked chutney. Do not skip it.
  7. Fold in the onion, green chilli, coriander leaves, salt, black salt and the raw mustard oil.
  8. Taste and serve at room temperature with litti, sattu paratha or a plate of khichdi.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Serve at room temperature and stir in a little fresh mustard oil before serving; it separates as it sits.
