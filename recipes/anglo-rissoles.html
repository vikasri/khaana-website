Anglo-Indian

Rissoles

minced meat folded into pastry crescents and fried, the Anglo-Indian tea plate staple

nut free
Prep40 min
Cook25 min
Active65 min
Plus30 min resting
Serves12
Difficultymoderate

Contains: milk, gluten, egg

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 12.

  • 300g Maida
  • 60g, cold and cubed Butter
  • 2, one for the dough and one for sealing Eggs
  • 400g minced Muttonbeef or chicken mince works equally
  • 2, finely chopped Onion
  • 2, boiled and mashed Potato
  • 1.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 3, chopped Green Chilli
  • a handful Coriander Leaves
  • 1 tbsp Vinegar
  • 500ml for frying Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Rissoles, croquettes and cutlets are the three fried things on every Anglo-Indian tea table. This is the pastry one.
  • The filling must be completely cold and quite dry before it goes near the pastry, or the seal fails in the oil.

Method

  1. Rub the butter into the flour with a pinch of salt, bind with one beaten egg and a little cold water, and rest the dough 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion until golden, add the mince and cook until it is dry and browned, 12 minutes.Dry. Any moisture left will burst the pastry.
  3. Stir in the mashed potato, pepper, green chilli, coriander and vinegar. Season and cool completely.
  4. Roll the dough thin, cut 10cm rounds, place a spoonful of filling on each and fold into a crescent.
  5. Seal the edges with beaten egg and crimp firmly with a fork.Crimp properly. A rissole that opens in the pan takes the oil with it.
  6. Fry in batches on medium until deep gold, about 4 minutes, and drain on paper.

Safe temperatures: Minced meat is done at 71°C / 160°F all the way through. Egg dishes are done at 71°C / 160°F; a whole egg when the yolk and white are both firm. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Crisp them in a hot oven rather than a microwave. They freeze well uncooked: fry from frozen, adding 2 minutes.
