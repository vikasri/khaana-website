Sev Tameta nu Shaak

soupy sweet-sharp tomato curry finished with crisp gram flour sev

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freenut free
Prep10 min
Cook20 min
Total30 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 600g, ripe, roughly chopped Tomato
  • 100g, pressed through a sev maker and fried Besanor simply use 100g ready-made nylon sev
  • 1 medium, finely chopped Onion
  • 4 cloves, crushed Garlic
  • 2, slit Green Chilli
  • 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 10 Curry Leaves
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1.5 tsp Chilli Powderkashmiri for colour without heat
  • 2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1.5 tbsp, grated Jaggery
  • 300ml Water
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Use properly ripe tomatoes; the gravy has nothing else to hide behind.
  • Keep the sev in a sealed box until the moment you serve. It goes limp within two minutes of hitting the gravy, which is the point, but only on the plate.
  • Make the gravy slightly thinner and sweeter than you would eat it plain — the sev soaks up liquid and mutes it.

Method

  1. Heat the oil and pop the mustard seeds, then add the cumin, curry leaves and asafoetida.
  2. Add the onion and garlic and fry 5 minutes until the onion is soft and translucent, not browned.
  3. Stir in the turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder off the heat for 10 seconds so they bloom without burning.Ground spices scorch in hot dry oil in under a minute — the tomatoes should follow immediately.
  4. Tip in the tomatoes, green chilli and salt and cook 8 minutes, pressing them down, until they collapse and the oil beads at the edges.
  5. Add the jaggery and the water and simmer 6 minutes into a loose, pourable gravy.It should be soupier than a normal shaak — thin it further if in doubt.
  6. Taste and balance: it wants to land sweet first, then sour, then hot.
  7. Ladle into bowls, pile the sev over the top and scatter the coriander.
  8. Serve at once with rotli or bhakri, while the sev is still crunchy.

Storage

The gravy keeps 3 days refrigerated and reheats well with a splash of water. Never store it with the sev in — add that fresh each time.
