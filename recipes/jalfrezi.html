Anglo-Indian

Jalfrezi

yesterday's roast turned into something better with chilli, onion and vinegar

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut free
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g cooked, torn into strips Chickenthe dish exists to use up cold roast meat; raw chicken works but misses the point
  • 3 medium, cut into petals Onion
  • 2, cut into strips Capsicum
  • 2, quartered Tomato
  • 4, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp, julienned Ginger
  • 6 cloves, sliced Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Vinegarthe Anglo-Indian signature; it is what separates this from a stir fry
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • 3 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Jalfrezi is a leftovers dish by design. The name comes from a Bengali word for hot frying, and the method is fast and dry.
  • Everything is cut large and stays distinct. It should not collapse into a curry.

Method

  1. Heat the oil until nearly smoking. Add the garlic and ginger and fry 30 seconds.
  2. Add the onion petals and capsicum and fry on high 4 minutes. They should char at the edges and stay crisp.High heat throughout. On low they sweat and go soft.
  3. Add the turmeric, cumin, chilli powder and green chillies. Toss 1 minute.
  4. Add the cooked meat and tomato and fry 4 minutes until heated through and glossy.
  5. Splash in the vinegar, let it hiss off, season and serve at once.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated but the vegetables soften. Reheat fast on high heat rather than gently.
