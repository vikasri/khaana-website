Hot and Sour Soup

thick pepper and vinegar soup with shredded vegetables, on every Indian Chinese menu

dairy freeegg freehealthiernut freevegetarian
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Active40 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: gluten, soy

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Ingredients Method Nutrition

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 150g, finely shredded Cabbage
  • 2, julienned Carrot
  • 1, julienned Capsicum
  • 100g, sliced Mushroom
  • 4, chopped Spring Onion
  • 1 tbsp, chopped Garlic
  • 1 tbsp, chopped Ginger
  • 3 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 3 tbsp Vinegarwhite vinegar, and more at the table
  • 2 tsp, coarsely crushed Black Pepperthe heat here is pepper, not chilli
  • 4 tbsp Cornflour
  • 1.2 litres Water
  • 2 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop

Before you start

  • Hot from pepper, sour from vinegar. Neither should be shy.
  • The soup is thickened until a spoon leaves a track. Thin hot and sour soup is just vegetable water.

Method

  1. Fry the garlic and ginger in the oil for 30 seconds, then add all the vegetables and toss on high 3 minutes.They should keep their bite. This is not a stew.
  2. Add the water, soy sauce and half the pepper. Simmer 8 minutes.
  3. Slake the cornflour in 150ml cold water and stir it in steadily until the soup thickens and turns glossy.Cold water. Dropped into hot liquid dry, cornflour lumps instantly.
  4. Add the vinegar and the rest of the pepper off the heat.Vinegar boiled hard loses its edge, which is the point of it.
  5. Check the salt, scatter spring onion, and serve with extra vinegar and chilli on the side.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. It thickens further as it cools, so loosen with hot water and re-sharpen with a splash of vinegar when reheating.
