Pahari

Kaapa

garhwali spinach thickened with gram flour, garlicky and eaten by the bowl with rice

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy freenut free
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Total45 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Allergens: none of the common ones

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 500g, washed and roughly chopped Spinach
  • 3 tbsp Besanthe thickener — no cream or dairy goes into kaapa
  • 3 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 8 cloves, crushed Garlic
  • 1 tbsp, finely chopped Ginger
  • 3, slit Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1/4 tsp Fenugreek Seedswhole seeds, not kasuri methi
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-free
  • 2, broken Dried Red Chillioptional
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 400ml Water
  • to taste Salt
  • 1/2, juiced Lemonoptional

Equipment

stovetop, kadhai, blender

Before you start

  • Wash the spinach in three changes of water. Hill spinach comes in gritty and grit in kaapa is unforgiveable.
  • Whisk the besan into 200ml cold water until completely smooth before you start cooking, and leave it aside. Lumps will not come out later.
  • Blanch and blend the spinach only if you want it smooth; many Garhwali homes leave it coarse and chopped.

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 90 seconds, drain and plunge into cold water to fix the colour.Ninety seconds, no more. Overcooked spinach goes olive and dull.
  2. Squeeze out the excess water and either chop it fine or pulse it briefly in a blender to a coarse puree.
  3. Heat the mustard oil until it smokes, cut the flame for a minute, then crackle the cumin and fenugreek seeds for 20 seconds.Fenugreek turns bitter quickly — the moment it reddens, move on.
  4. Add the hing, red chillies, garlic, ginger and green chillies and fry 2 minutes until the garlic is golden.
  5. Stir in the turmeric and coriander powder, then pour in the smooth besan slurry, whisking continuously.
  6. Cook the besan mixture on a low flame for 6-8 minutes until it thickens and stops smelling of raw flour.Raw besan is the single most common fault in kaapa. Give it the full 8 minutes.
  7. Add the spinach, the remaining 200ml water and salt. Simmer uncovered 10 minutes until it is thick enough to hold a ridge from the spoon.
  8. Finish with the lemon juice off the heat and serve with hot rice.

Storage

Keeps 2 days refrigerated. Reheat gently with a splash of water; the colour dulls on day two but the flavour improves.
