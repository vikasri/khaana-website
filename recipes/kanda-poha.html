Maharashtrian

Kanda Poha

flattened rice steamed soft with onion, peanuts and curry leaves, breakfast in fifteen minutes

vegetarianvegangluten freedairy free
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Total25 min
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Contains: nuts

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 150g (about 2 cups), thick variety Pohathin poha turns to paste; buy the thick jada poha
  • 2 medium, finely chopped Onion
  • 50g, raw Peanut
  • 3 tbsp Groundnut Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seedsoptional
  • 2, slit lengthways Green Chilli
  • 10 Curry Leaves
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetidause compounded-free hing to keep this gluten-freeoptional
  • 1 tsp SugarMaharashtrian poha is faintly sweet
  • 1 small, cut into 1cm dice Potatomakes it kanda-batata pohaoptional
  • 1 Lemon
  • 3 tbsp, chopped Coriander Leavesoptional
  • 2 tbsp, freshly grated Coconutoptional
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Before you start

  • Rinse the poha in a colander under running water, tossing with your fingers, then leave it to stand 10 minutes. Never soak it in a bowl of water.
  • Chop the onion fine and have everything within reach. Once the mustard seeds pop the dish takes six minutes.

Method

  1. Rinse the poha in a colander until the grains soften, then leave it to drain and swell for 10 minutes.Squeeze one grain: it should crush cleanly. Hard in the middle means another sprinkle of water; mushy means you soaked it.
  2. Toss the drained poha with the turmeric, sugar and salt so every grain turns yellow. Do this with your fingertips, not a spoon.
  3. Fry the peanuts in the oil over medium heat for 3 minutes until they darken and smell toasted, then lift them out.
  4. Into the same oil add the mustard seeds and wait for them to pop, then the cumin, green chilli, curry leaves and hing.Stand back. Wet curry leaves spit.
  5. Add the diced potato, if using, cover and cook 6 minutes until tender.
  6. Add the onion and cook 4 minutes until soft and translucent but not browned.Browned onion makes the poha taste of fried onion instead of fresh.
  7. Fold in the poha and the fried peanuts, lower the heat, cover and steam 3 minutes.
  8. Turn off the heat, squeeze over the lemon and scatter with coriander and grated coconut. Serve at once.

Storage

Poha is a fifteen-minute dish and does not keep. It dries out within a couple of hours. If you must, refrigerate 1 day and revive it with a sprinkle of water, covered, over low heat.
