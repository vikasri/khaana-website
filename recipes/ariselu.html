Andhra

Ariselu

rice flour and jaggery discs fried for Sankranti, chewy and dark

dairy freeegg freegluten freeno onion garlicnut freeveganvegetarian
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep40 min
Cook30 min
Active70 min
Plus4 hr resting
Serves12
Difficultyadvanced

Start the day before. The resting alone is 4 hr, against 70 min of actual work.

Contains: sesame

Ingredients

Quantities for 12.

  • 400g, soaked 4 hours then dried and ground Ricethe flour must be slightly damp, which is why it is ground at home
  • 300g Jaggery
  • 3 tbsp Sesame
  • 6 pods, seeds ground Cardamom
  • 500ml for frying Neutral Oil
  • 100ml Water

Cookware

stovetop, kadhai

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • The jaggery syrup stage decides everything. Too soft and the discs dissolve in the oil; too hard and they are inedible.
  • Test it: a drop of syrup in cold water should form a ball you can roll between your fingers.

Method

  1. Melt the jaggery with the water and boil to the soft-ball stage, about 8 minutes.Drop a little into cold water. It should gather into a soft ball, not a thread and not a brittle sheet.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in the rice flour, sesame and cardamom quickly, while the syrup is still hot.
  3. Knead into a soft dough while just cool enough to handle. Rest it 4 hours or overnight.The rest lets the flour drink the syrup. Skipping it gives a hard ariselu.
  4. Pat portions into 8cm discs between two greased sheets of plastic.
  5. Fry one at a time on medium, spooning oil over the top, until deep brown, about 3 minutes.One at a time. They are fragile and stick to each other.
  6. Press each one between two flat spoons as it comes out to squeeze the oil back.

Storage

Keeps 2 weeks in an airtight tin at room temperature and firms up over the first few days, which is normal.
