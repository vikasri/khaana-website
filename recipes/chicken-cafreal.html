Chicken Cafreal

chicken marinated green with coriander, chilli and vinegar, then fried dry

dairy freeegg freegluten freenut free
Ingredients Method Nutrition
Prep20 min
Cook35 min
Active55 min
Plus4 hr marinating
Serves4
Difficultyeasy

Start the day before. The marinating alone is 4 hr, against 55 min of actual work.

Allergens: none of the ten listed below

Ingredients

Quantities for 4.

  • 800g, legs and thighs, skin off, slashed to the bone Chickenon the bone only, breast dries out
  • 2 large bunches, stalks included Coriander Leavesthe stalks carry most of the flavour
  • 8, roughly chopped Green Chilli
  • 10 cloves Garlic
  • 4cm piece Ginger
  • 1.5 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 6 Cloves
  • 2cm stick Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 3 tbsp Vinegar
  • 1, juiced Lemon
  • 1 tsp Sugaroptional
  • 2 medium, quartered Potatofried alongside, the standard accompanimentoptional
  • 4 tbsp Neutral Oil
  • to taste Salt

Cookware

stovetop, blender, kadhai

Checked against the ingredient list for ten allergens: milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, mustard, soy and gluten. Brands vary, so read the label on anything new to you.

Before you start

  • Marinate the chicken at least 4 hours, ideally overnight. Cafreal is a marinade dish. An hour gives you green chicken with plain meat underneath.
  • Slash each piece to the bone in two or three places so the paste gets inside.
  • Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking so it cooks evenly.

Method

  1. Grind the coriander with stalks, green chillies, garlic, ginger, cumin, pepper, cloves, cinnamon, turmeric, vinegar, lemon juice, sugar and 1 tsp salt to a thick green paste. Use no water.Water dulls the colour and thins the marinade. The vinegar and lemon are enough liquid.
  2. Rub the paste hard into the slashed chicken, working it into the cuts. Cover and refrigerate 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Heat the oil in a heavy kadhai and lay in the chicken in a single layer, scraping the marinade back into the bowl.
  4. Fry 4 minutes a side over medium-high heat until the surface is seared and patchy brown.Sear first, marinade later. If you tip everything in together the paste burns before the chicken cooks.
  5. Add the reserved marinade and 4 tbsp water, cover, and cook on low for 20 minutes, turning the pieces twice.
  6. Meanwhile fry the quartered potatoes in a separate pan until gold and cooked through, about 12 minutes.
  7. Uncover, raise the heat and cook 5-6 minutes more until the sauce has reduced to a thick coating that clings to the chicken.Cafreal is a dry dish. If there is a pool of gravy in the pan, keep going.
  8. Check with a knife at the thickest part of the thigh. The juices should run clear. Squeeze over lemon and serve with the potatoes and pav.

Safe temperatures: Chicken and duck are done at 74°C / 165°F, taken at the thickest part and clear of the bone. Reheat leftovers to 74°C / 165°F. FoodSafety.gov

Storage

Keeps 3 days refrigerated. Reheat in a covered pan with 2 tbsp water over low heat; the microwave makes it stringy. The marinade freezes well for 2 months.
